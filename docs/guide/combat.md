A step only changes the row; it never makes a write-back itself (lint: "what a fight row's sheet hears is read once a command"). A test in `tests/fight.test.ts`, the command in `randomCommand` (`tests/models/fight-play.ts`), and its words in `step` in `scripts/charts/fight.ts`.

- An ability: an entry with its kind (strike, shot, blast, curse, support), made by `abilityOf` (`src/core/registry/ability-kinds.ts`), which fills cost, range, shape, delivery and the kind's recommended formula; give only what differs. - A terrain: a member of `Terrain` and its row in `TERRAIN` (`space.ts`: its character, whether a body may stand on it, its cost to enter, whether it stops a line and sight, whether it is cover; the typecheck asks), with a case in `tests/space.test.ts` on a drawn grid.
