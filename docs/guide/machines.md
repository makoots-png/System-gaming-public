# Guide: machines and their charts

## Machines and their charts

`docs/state-machines.md` charts every explicit machine: the fight and a channel (`fight.ts`, `fight-channel.ts`), a stacking status's count (`stacks.ts`), a trigger's limit (`triggers.ts`), a delayed effect (`delayed.ts`), a zone of the kits (`zones.ts`), a timed summon (`summons.ts`), a limited use (`uses.ts`), wave 2's reaction, edge, push, stealth, ward and turn extras (`fight-react.ts`, `standing.ts`, `space.ts`, `timeline.ts`; one file of views, `scripts/charts/wave2.ts`), a gate, the room link, a hunter's life, the penalty, the Job Change chain and a coin call (`coin.ts`), first-run setup (`onboarding.ts`), the battle map's life on the page (`combat/map-mount.ts`), a gesture on it (`combat/gesture.ts`) and what it has chosen (`choose`, `map-view.ts`), a shadow (`hunter/shadows.ts`) and a corpse's extraction (`combat/extraction.ts`), a quest (`hunter/quests.ts`), the room server's database (`room-server.ts`) and the app update (`upgrade.ts`), a message in the outbox (`sync/outbox.ts`), a room doc the GM publishes (`sync/publish.ts`), a Create or Join tap from the tap to its answer (`sync/attempt.ts`), the store's life (`store-life.ts`), a run of the odds engine (`combat/odds.ts`), a hunter's personal gates (`gates/solo.ts` `soloTick`), a solo run on this phone from the tap to its window (`gates/solo-run.ts`), a solo run waiting for the GM (`gates/solo-wait.ts`), a story on the table (`story/run.ts`), an offer in the room with the giver's, taker's and GM's records of it (`trade/offer.ts`), a list's open "more info" detail (`detail-open.ts`), and the windows on screen, waiting and going (`windows.ts`). `bun run charts` walks the real reducers with the event lists in `tests/models/` (the fight has no end of states, so its chart replays the random play of `tests/models/fight-play.ts`), and `tests/statecharts.test.ts` fails when the file is stale or a state has no box. Read a machine's chart before you change it. A new machine gets a view in `scripts/charts/` and an entry in `MACHINES` in `scripts/statecharts.ts`.
