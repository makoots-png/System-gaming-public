## Recipes

Where a step says "the typecheck asks", `bun run typecheck` fails until it is done.

- A write a stray tap should be able to stop: `ui.toast(tag, msg, { hold: { ms, act, undo } })` runs the one write `act` as the toast ends, never after Undo (Give…'s, `gm.js` `giveFrom`); never a second path for the same write. A held toast takes no `key`, so it never adds up with another. Each toast's timer and held write are kept in the `toasts` Map of `ui.js` until it goes, so the write runs at most once.
- An icon: a row of `ICONS` in `src/core/registry/icons.ts` (a key of lowercase letters and hyphens, at most `ICON_KEY_MAX`; its group; its label; its path drawn by the style rules in the file's header). Run `bun run test` (`tests/icons.test.ts` reads every entry). A key once shipped is never renamed: change its drawing instead. Every point of a path sits inside 2 to 22, and a new icon needs its row in `src/core/registry/icons-credit.md` (who drew it, its source, what was changed). What a thing shows when no icon is picked is a cell of a table in `src/core/registry/icon-defaults.ts` (`monsterIcon`, `itemIcon`, `statusIcon`, `opIcon`, `abilityIcon`). A monster, an item and a status definition each carry an optional `icon` a GM picked, read by `isIconKey` in its one parser (`parseMonster`, `parseItem`, `defOf`): an unknown key is dropped and the default shows; two items that differ only by icon do not stack (`DISTINGUISHES.icon`). A fight row's origin carries a monster's pick (`Origin.icon`, set by `monsterRows`, read by `originOf`), and the fight doc carries each row's picture as `ic`, worked out once on the GM's phone by `rowIcon` (`src/core/combat/row-icon.ts`: a foe's pick or default, a hunter's emblem or none), so a player's phone computes nothing (the worst doc keeps 944 characters under `DOC_MAX`, held by its test). Every screen draws through `icon` and `sprite` in `src/views/icons.ts`, at the size tokens on `:root`. A GM picks one in the monster, item and status makers: the Icon row is `iconField` in `src/views/maker.js`, its state the machine of `src/core/icon-pick.ts` (`iconPickStep`; `iconShown` and `iconWords` say which picture shows and why); Default stores no key. See the whole set side by side with `bun scripts/ui-shots.ts --serve 3261 --out <dir> --only icons`. A hunter's emblem is an icon (`isIconKey`, `iconDef`, `iconsOf('emblem')`).
- A monster: a row of `SEEDS` in `src/core/registry/monsters.ts`: key, name, rank, type, archetype, kind, tweak (`hp` a factor, `stats` points, `traits` named body changes), abilities as `MonsterSpec`s, notes, description. Its sheet is `monsterSheet` from those (`src/core/hunter/derive.ts`: its archetype's kit, `kitGear`, the same at every rank; max HP, armor and resist times its kind's and its traits' factors, `RULES.fight.kinds` and `RULES.fight.traits`, max HP and power times its archetype's, `RULES.fight.archetypes`; balance plan section 1); every monster has its archetype's attack (`ARCHETYPE_ATTACKS`). A GM's monster goes through `parseMonster` (a new field and its bound go there): its `abilities`, its starting `statuses` (a key and rounds, on its row when it joins a fight through `monsterRows`), and its `loot` (listed when a fight it lost ends, `lootOf`). A GM makes one in the monster maker (`bestiary.make`, `bestiary.js`), which shows the HP, Armor, Resist and Speed its rank, archetype, kind and ticked traits give. A monster's XP is `monsterXp` (absent, the XP table's for its rank, kind and traits). Whether a fallen monster may be extracted is its own `extractable`, else its type's: `MONSTER_TYPES` in `src/core/registry/monster-types.ts` (the sixteen types; a type the GM typed that is none of them counts as yes); a new type is a row there and its picture in `icon-defaults.ts` (`tests/monster-types.test.ts`).
- A monster trait: an entry of `RULES.fight.traits` (`src/core/rules.ts`: its `hp`, `armor` and `resist` factors, its `speed` percent and its `xp` factor, each with its reason), its name in `TRAIT_LABEL` (`registry/monsters.ts`; the typecheck asks), so the parser keeps it, the maker shows its chip and the rules reference its row; a case in `tests/monsters.test.ts` ("a trait changes a monster's body by its rule").
- An op kind in the makers: a new member of `Op` is a key of `OP_KIND_SET` (`effect-ops.ts`; the typecheck asks), so the status maker offers it and the round-trip test in `tests/e2e/makers.e2e.ts` fails until its row in `maker.js` reads and writes it (`KIND_LABEL`, then `FORMULA_KINDS`, `NUMBERS` (a bound from the rules in `specOf`) or `PLAIN_KINDS`, or its own branch in `rowOf`, `checkRow`, `rowHtml` and `blank`). A `stat` row and a `sheet` row on a key of `SHEET_FORMULA_KEYS` are lasting rows (`lasting`, item-stats 6.2): a formula field checked by `checkLasting` (`bareRefusal`'s words, a written number within its `specOf` bound) with its words on a sample rank C hunter; a `sheet` row on `exposed`, `range` or sight is a number row, and a lasting row moved to one keeps only a written number (`toNumberRow`). A use op or a land op is also listed in `ROW_KINDS.skill` or `ROW_KINDS.land`.
- A kits field in a maker (a price or gate kind, a trigger event, a stack rule's field, an ability's wave 2 field): its control in `kit-parts.js` (`statusBlocks` for a def, `abilityBlocks` for an ability: the row's HTML, its reader that answers exactly what the parser gives back, its summary line), and a step in `tests/e2e/kits.e2e.ts` that saves it and reads it back; the examples' round trip there covers every field an example holds.
- A maker: open it with `app.api.maker.open({ tag, title, save, build })`; `build(body, shell)` fills the body from `chips`, `bonus`, `effects` (the effects editor: one row per op, every kind of `OP_KINDS` for a status, its amount a text read by `parseFormula` with its error on the row, by rank where the op has tiers), and `ability` (the ability form: the formula field, `formulaField` in maker.js, then cost, reach, how it is avoided and the channel, each a collapsed `.mk-group`) and answers `{ save, preview, blocked }`. An ability is made in the skill maker only; the enemy maker's ability rows open it. `preview` gives `describeStatus` or `describeItem` lines, redrawn on every change. `save` runs the kind's parser, answers what it gives back, and treats a difference from the form as a bug in the form. Mark the part of the form below the first screen `data-later`, so the window opens in the tap's frame. No game rule goes in `maker.js`. Copy `forge.js` (a status), `loot.js` (an item), `skills.js` (a skill) or `bestiary.js` (a monster), and list the kind in the Workshop (`workshop.js`); a test in `tests/e2e/makers.e2e.ts` drives it through the UI and reads the result from the save and the game.
- A kind of custom content: its type and a parser in core that gives a wrong field its default and refuses only what has no name (`parseItem` is the model), run at load, by the maker and on a room message; its list in `library` (`save.ts`: `newSave`, a repair line, a cap in `LIMITS`); its line in `Pack`, `PACK_KINDS`, `PACK_WORDS` (its words where a pack is shared or read; the typecheck asks), its walk in `STATUS_REFS` (`src/core/status-refs.ts`), `packOf`, `parsePack` and `mergePack` (`src/core/pack.ts`; a case in `tests/pack.test.ts`); its maker through `app.api.maker.open` (copy one of the four in the maker recipe); its section in the Workshop (`workshop.js`: New, Edit, Duplicate, Delete, Share); a case in the parser's test and a browser step in `tests/e2e/makers.e2e.ts`.
- A catch: delete the try when the call can fail only by a bug (app.js then draws the failed part and logs it); otherwise read the error (`reported(where, e)`, or tell the player by its kind), throw it again, or say in a comment of four words or more why the error is not needed (a fallback the browser forces, a typed failure the player is told). A `let` a view keeps for the page is named in the lint "every let a view keeps" with what it holds; two flags that change together are one union or a machine in core.
- A view or panel: `app.registerView` or `app.registerPanel` with a literal `id` in a module listed in `MODULES` in `src/app.js`; shapes and defaults in `src/views/app.ts`. The app draws the shown view after each change of the save; for a change outside it (a room doc, the view's own memory) the view calls `app.redraw()`. `app.go(id)` is a navigation only, to a literal id (a lint): a redraw through it, landing between a tab's tap and its `hashchange`, undoes the tap.
- A screen's look: a colour or a control height is a token (`--edge` for a field or button edge, `--control-h` for 44 px, `--z-toast`), never a literal. A button is plain, `button.primary` (filled; one per window, the action it exists for), `button.danger` (red at rest) or `button.quiet` (text only). A one-of-n choice is a `.seg` row of buttons with `aria-pressed` or `aria-selected` (a many-of-n chip is `.mk-chip`). An icon button is `.icon-btn`. A form longer than a screen ends in `.dlg-actions`, Cancel left and the action right. News that needs no answer is `ui.toast(tag, msg)`, never a window; the fight's news (a use, a refusal, a turn) is `ui.news(msg, kind)`, which the map's line takes while a map shows a fight (`ui.newsLine`, `map.js`) and is a toast otherwise. A new screen gets a step in `scripts/ui-shots.ts`, and a test in `tests/e2e/look.e2e.ts` for what it must show at 360 or 390 px.
- A motion: call a move from `src/views/motion.ts` after the view draws (`enter`, `dismiss`, `pop`, `shake`, `flash`, `fadeOff`, `slideTo`, `fillTo`, `numberTo`, `tumble`, `burst`, `progress`); never await one before a command. A time or an easing is a token on `:root` in `public/app.css`, a new move a function in `motion.ts` built on `play` (only `transform` and `opacity`), a burst kind a row of `BURSTS`. A view writes no time, no `@keyframes` and no `.animate(` (the lint tests ask). After a change to a fight screen, run `tests/e2e/feel.e2e.ts`.
- More info on a thing: a new kind is a member of `Thing` and its case in `detailOf` (`src/core/detail.ts`; the typecheck asks), with a case in `tests/detail.test.ts`, its lines from core's own sentence functions. A view puts `app.api.detail.control(list, key)` on the thing's button, `app.api.detail.panel(list, key, thing)` right after it in its markup, and calls `app.api.detail.wire(root, redraw)` once; the open one is kept by the component, so a view that redraws draws it open again. A sentence goes in core, never in the view.
- An event: its entry in `AppEvents` (`src/core/events.ts`), then `app.emit` and `app.on`. An API member: its type in `src/core/api.ts`, its registration in the module, and a caller in another module.
- A recipe item, one a GM could make in the Workshop and the library holds as an example (`RECIPE_ITEMS` in `src/core/registry/items.ts`, spread into `LOOT_ITEMS`; an `ItemDef` of a worn type may hold `worn` and `active`): size it by `itemSpend` (`item-budget.ts`) so it sits in its own grade, since the budget is small (Rare+ is 4.4 points) and scaling amounts are read on the build that favours them; say in a comment over it how its spend adds up, and add no test of its own, since `tests/items.test.ts` ("the recipe items") reads each one back and checks `gradeOfSpend` equals its grade. The five, with the shape each is made of and the part the plan's amounts or shapes left out:
  - Thornmail (Epic+): a `struck` trigger to the striker, `damage` magical `1 + 0.01 * armor`; Vitality 1. Its healing cut (a `share` op on `healed`) is left out until `itemSpend` prices one.
  - Spellblade (Rare): a `used` trigger on the tag `spell` that puts on a one-stack status leaving on `hitDealt`, and a `hitDealt` trigger `need`ing that status with a `damage` of `2 + 0.02 * str`. A GM tags a skill `spell` for it to ready the blade.
  - Healer's Charm (Rare): Presence 2 and a `kill` trigger to self healing `0.05 * sup`. A per-round `heal` op in a worn status is unweighed, so the heal is a trigger. `healPower` (unit 1) is not built, so no "+x% heals".
  - Bulwark Ring (Rare+): an `active` support ability on self, `shield` `10 + 0.05 * hpmax`, one use in 8 rounds. The plan's `10 + 0.2 * hpmax` every 3 rounds spends 23.2.
  - Warden's Plate (Epic-): a heavy chest piece with a worn `sheet` op `armor` of `0.015 * hpmax`.
