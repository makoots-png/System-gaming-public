## Recipes

Where a step says "the typecheck asks", `bun run typecheck` fails until it is done.

- A fact the System remembers: a member of `Fact` (`src/core/hunter/facts.ts`), its row in `FACT_FIELDS` (the typecheck asks; a field's spec is its bound) and `KEPT`, its sentence in `FACT_LINES` (`chronicle-view.ts`; the typecheck asks) with its sample in `tests/chronicle-view.test.ts`, its rows in `TALLY` (`chronicle.ts`) if it counts toward a lifetime number, and its one writer: the core step that does the thing calls `record(h, fact)` in that step, never after it (the store's `noticed` takes it in the same write). A case in `tests/chronicle.test.ts` written from the rule.
- A built-in feat: an entry of `ENTRIES` in `src/core/registry/feats.ts` (a key no other holds, its idea's F number, its count over today's facts, when it is shown, its window's words through humanizer and unslop, a reward that exists today); `tests/feats.test.ts` reads every one back. A count of a new fact field needs only the field (the fact recipe); a new window is a member of `WITHINS` and its line in `markOf`.
- A built-in award for roleplay: an entry of `BUILT_IN_BUNDLES` (`src/core/registry/bundles.ts`, a `seed-` key, its moment, a few words of when, `rare: true` past 2 points, its window's words), with a comment saying why its size is what it is; `tests/bundles.test.ts` reads every one. A new moment is a member of `MOMENTS` with its heading in `MOMENT_WORDS` and its chip in `MOMENT_CHIPS` (the typecheck asks).
