## Recipes

Where a step says "the typecheck asks", `bun run typecheck` fails until it is done.

- A derived number: a field of `Sheet` and its label in `SHEET_LABELS` (`src/core/hunter/derive.ts`; the typecheck asks), its formula in `derive`, its reason in `docs/agent-handoff/rw/design.md` section 2.2 and its row in `tests/derive.test.ts`. A formula may then name it.
- A rule of the day's time (when a day turns, a due time, when something is due): a function of `src/core/day.ts` (or of the module that owns the thing, as `overdue` in `quests.ts`) that takes the time as an argument and reads local time only through `dayKey` and `nextReset`, with its cases in `tests/day.test.ts` in zones whose clocks change (set `process.env.TZ` in the test, back in `afterEach`). Something new that falls due at a time is a term of `nextWake`, so the one timer wakes for it.
- A state or event of the life, penalty, chain, app update, store or doc publish machine: its cells in that file's `TABLE` (`ROW` for the penalty; the typecheck asks), the walk's events in `tests/models/` and its words or arrow in `scripts/charts/`.
- A job: an entry with exactly three steps in `src/core/registry/jobs.ts`. It may extract shadows when it is in the rules' `extractJobs`.
- An item: `SHOP_ITEMS`, `LOOT_ITEMS` or `STARTER_GEAR` in `src/core/registry/items.ts`; leave out `value` to price it by the rule. Gear has `weight`, `armor` and `resist`; a weapon has `attack` (`strike` or `shot`) and its dice, which `weaponAttack` makes the hunter's attack. A consumable's `effects` are ops; in a fight it is the hunter's ability (`itemAbility`, `abilities.ts`, carrying every use op `isUseOp` names), used from the card or sent as `cast`. A consumable with `revive` (the Resurrection Crystal) raises its dead user outside a fight (`useItem`), is never sold back (`sellable`) and never in a loot roll (`lootPool`); the item maker's box for it is a GM's only. An item from outside data goes through `parseItem` (a new field and its bound go there and in `ITEM_BOUNDS`).
- A skill: an entry of `SKILL_SEEDS` in `src/core/registry/skills.ts`, an active one also in `SKILL_ABILITIES` (`abilities.ts`; the typecheck asks); a passive holds a `bonus`. A skill from outside data goes through `parseSkill` (a new field and its bound go there and in `SKILL_BOUNDS`); the GM's own are `library.skills`. A GM makes one in the skill maker (`skills.make`, `skills.js`) and gives it with `skills.give`: a hunter on this phone gets a copy, a player an `award` holding that skill alone.
- A path or perk: `src/core/registry/paths.ts`, six tiers of two perks, each worth its tier's budget.
- A reward field: the type in `Rewards`, its default in `newRewards` and its reader in `parseRewards` (`src/core/rewards.ts`, bounds in `REWARD_BOUNDS`; the typecheck asks for all three), its words in `rewardLines` (the quest card) and `grantLines` (the reward window and the editor's preview), what it gives in `grant` (`src/core/hunter/grant.ts`, in its one step, adding what it gave to the window's lines), a case in its describe of `tests/rewards.test.ts`, and its row in the reward editor (`reward-editor.js`: a chip in `ADD`, a cap in `CAP`, `blank`, `rowsOf`, `raw`, `rowHtml`). Cases in `tests/rewards.test.ts`; a browser step in `tests/e2e/reward-room.e2e.ts`.
- A control that undoes another (equip and unequip, give and take back): its round-trip case beside the function's own, in the module's test (`tests/inventory.test.ts`, `tests/record.test.ts`, `tests/party.test.ts`, `tests/shadows.test.ts`, `tests/progress.test.ts`), doing then undoing and comparing the whole value; where the undo is not exact (a sale pays half), the test asserts the stated difference. The pairs and where each is tested are listed in `docs/agent-handoff/final/notes-roundtrip.md`.
