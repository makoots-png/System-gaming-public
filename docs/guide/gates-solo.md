## Recipes

Where a step says "the typecheck asks", `bun run typecheck` fails until it is done.

- A gate status or event: `GATE_STATUSES` or `GateEvent` in `src/core/gates/gate.ts`, and its cells in `TABLE` (the typecheck asks). Rows in `tests/gate.test.ts`, the event in `WALK_EVENTS` (`tests/models/gate-events.ts`). `gates.js` shows its button when `can(g, type)` and applies it with a step of `src/core/gates/world.ts` (`onGate` runs the event and logs it); words in `STATUS_LABEL` and `scripts/charts/gate.ts`. XP is paid only by `xpDue` and `fightAward`.
- A gate name or spawn weight: `gateNames` and `gateSpawn` in `src/core/rules.ts` (weights are percentages); the roll is `rollGate` in `src/core/gates/spawn.ts`.
