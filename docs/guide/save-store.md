## Recipes

Where a step says "the typecheck asks", `bun run typecheck` fails until it is done.

- A field of a saved thing: the type and its default in `save.ts`, or in `hunter/hunter.ts` for a hunter and what it holds (the typecheck asks), a repair line if it has a range or allowed values, a case in `tests/save.test.ts`. A field a view adds and removes is optional with no default. Level, XP or points go in `Progress` (`progress.ts`), HP, mana, fatigue or life in `Vitals` (`vitals.ts`), a penalty field in `Penalty` and `parsePenalty`, a gate field in `GateFields`, `newGate`, `fieldsOf` and `parseGate`, an item, skill or monster field in its registry's type and parser, each with a case in its own test. A reward field has its own recipe below.
- An id of a new kind of thing: `Id<'Name'>` in `src/core/ids.ts` with its parser (a save id: its prefix in `SaveIds`, made by the store's `id` or `saveId`, read by `parseSaveId`). Never a brand of its own, and a branded name once (a lint test). A field that names a thing holds its id, never its name; a hunter here or in the room is a `HunterRef`. A fact a view would guess from words (a roll's kind, a monster made by the spawn) is a field set where the thing is made.
- A write to the save from a view: a pure function in the core module that owns that state (the table above) answering the next value or `refuse(why)`, then `store.apply(reason, atHunter(hid, x => op(x, args)))` (`atLibrary`, `atSettings`, or a step on the whole save; `both(...)` chains steps into one write, all or nothing). Reserve an id with `store.id` before the step; read the clock in the view. The lint "a view writes the save only through store.apply" counts anything else. Read its answer: `if (!ui.wrote(tag, store.apply(...), words)) return;` stops what follows a refused write and tells the player by its kind (`words`: the refusals the step's core module words); the lint "a view reads the answer of every write to the save" names any call that drops it, and lists with its reason the few whose refusal needs nothing.

## Saves and roles

A save has `schema: 4` under `hunter-system-v4`. A tab whose stored save a newer app wrote, or whose unsaved change another tab's save overtook, is read-only: `store.apply` refuses every change (`read-only`) and a banner says why; a restore, an import or a reset makes it ready again. An older save (schema 1 to 3, under `hunter-system-v3`, `-v2` or `-v1`) is not carried over: its text is kept in the backups, the player is told, and the app starts a new campaign; its old key is left as it was. A campaign file or a hunter from before schema 4 is refused at import. A stored text that is not a save is kept in the backups and the player is told. In a room the phone that holds the room's GM key is the GM; `settings.mode` is only what a phone chose while alone.
