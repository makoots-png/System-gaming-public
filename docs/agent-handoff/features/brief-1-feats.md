## The goal, in the owner's words

The feature, as he picked it: "The System acts on its own": feats noticed without the GM's hand (a count of kills, a survival at 1 HP, a streak) that grant hidden quests and titles, made by the GM in a maker as a status's triggers are. And on Nov 18, after reading the first list of ideas: "Give me some that I should administer on my own when I see instances of roleplay. Build the functionalities necessary through pstack, architecture first, and think about what those new functionalities would allow for us to do." His standing words: "I want it perfect, I do not care about time." "Everything must be explicit. Everything." "Architecture first." "For all things we must fix the problem and not the symptoms."

The problem: nothing in the game remembers what a hunter has done. A fight ends and only its totals reach the sheet; a streak is one number; no rule can say "the third time", "without resting", "while nobody else stood". So the System cannot notice anything, and the GM has no quick way to mark a moment at the table.

## What the design must answer (architecture first: the data shape before any rule)

1. **What the game remembers about a hunter.** This is the foundation and the real decision. Weigh at least three shapes against the ideas files' entries, by counting how many entries each can express and what each costs in the save, the sheet push (`DOC_MAX`-style bounds: do the arithmetic) and a room message: (a) named counters on the hunter (a tally); (b) a chronicle, an append-only list of typed facts with their time, from which counts are derived and which a player can read; (c) a tally plus a bounded chronicle of notable facts. Name the fact types as a union (a kill with its rank, type and kind; a fight's end with that row's summary; a solo run's end; a quest's end; a daily's day; a life event; a roll's natural; a shop act; a long rest; an award by the GM). Say what is counted where: one writer per fact, in the same write as the thing it records (`fought` and the fight's settle, `soloRan`, `finishQuest`, `rollover`, the life machine's callers, `grant`), never a second write after.
2. **Where a fact is made when the fight is on the GM's phone and the hunter is on a player's.** The player's phone holds the hunter; the GM's phone holds the fight. A player's totals already travel as a room message in the fight's write. Design how a fight's summary for a player's row reaches that hunter's phone once (a new message by the recipe, or a field of one that exists), and who evaluates a feat (the phone that holds the hunter is the lead's lean: say why or why not). Idempotent by the event's id.
3. **A feat as content.** A feat is custom content (AGENTS.md's recipe): its type, one parser, its list in the library with a cap, pack support, a maker, a Workshop section, built-ins shipped in a registry. Its condition is the heart: weigh (a) a small typed tree of conditions over facts and counts, (b) the formula language of `formula.ts` given a scope of counts (`count(kill.E) >= 100`), (c) a fixed list of condition kinds with numbers. Pick by what a GM can make in a maker on a phone and what the ideas files need. A feat has states (hidden, shown with progress, earned, earned and repeatable?): draw the machine on a hunter's feat, every cell. What a feat grants is a `Rewards` (through `grant`, one write with the feat's earned mark and its log line) and may issue a quest.
4. **A quest that counts by itself** (the ideas file's N8): an objective moved by facts, a hidden quest a feat issues with no GM tap, what failing costs (N9) as something the app applies. Say how this sits in the quest machine without a second quest system.
5. **The GM's awards for roleplay.** At the table the GM sees a moment and marks it in two taps: pick the hunter (this phone's or a player's), pick from a shelf of awards (built-ins from the ideas file, the GM's own), done; the player's phone shows the System's window. Design it as the same content as a feat with no condition (given by hand), or as a reward bundle with a window line, whichever removes a concept; say which and why. It is also a fact in the chronicle, so the System can notice "commended three times in one session". Where it lives on the GM's screens (the GM view, a fight's card, the Table feed), and its room message (an award travels today: reuse it if it can carry the window's line).
6. **What a title can do** (N7): today a title is a name and a stat bonus. Say what the smallest general extension is (a title that carries a worn status, as a passive skill does through `wornStatuses`, would give titles every op a status has, with no new mechanism) and what that unlocks.
7. **Evaluation.** When feats are checked (in the write that adds a fact, by a pure step: `noticed(save, hunter, facts)` answering the feats earned and the next save), its cost (how many feats, how many facts, per write: measure or bound it), and that a feat earned is earned once however many times the write is replayed.
8. **What the player sees**: a chronicle or record page, hidden feats as "???" or not at all (weigh), progress toward a shown feat, the window when one is earned, and the titles it gave. What the GM sees of a player's feats on the board.
9. **What these capabilities would let us do** that nobody has asked for yet. The owner asked for this by name. Think forward from the data shape, alone and in combination: a hunter's chronicle the System writes as prose, rivalries noticed between two hunters, a campaign's legends, titles the dead leave, a System with a temperament, a recap at a session's start, feats for the GM, content packs of feats shared between tables, a record that survives a hunter's death. For each: what it needs beyond the base, how much a table would feel it, and whether the base as designed carries it without a rewrite. Rank them. This section decides between otherwise equal data shapes: choose the shape that keeps the most of these open.
10. **The cut.** The whole is large. Propose the order as verifiable units, each landing whole with its tests and leaving the app working: the lead expects something like (1) the facts and their writers, (2) the feat's type, parser, machine and evaluation with the built-ins that need nothing else, (3) the room's part, (4) the maker and Workshop, (5) the GM's awards, (6) the player's and GM's screens, (7) counting quests and titles with worn statuses. Say what each unit deletes or simplifies on the way (a streak that becomes a derived count, `Gate.fought`).
11. The rule numbers (caps, bounds) each with its reason; the files and tests by name per unit; the choices the owner might want otherwise; the questions only he can answer (few).
