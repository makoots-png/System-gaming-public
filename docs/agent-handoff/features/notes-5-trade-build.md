## Step 1: `gates/solo-wait.ts` becomes `sync/asked.ts` (commit 1d99b45)

- `Asked` is one record of "a letter waiting for the GM's phone's answer", its kind a union: `solo` (gate, name) or `stash` (a `StashAsk`). `settings.sync.runs` is now `settings.sync.asked`, the run's `run` field is the ask's `id`, `no-run` is `no-ask`. Names: `askPosted`, `askTaken`, `askedGone`, `askAnswered`, `askDismissed`, `askedStep`, `ASKED_*`; the solo run's words (`WAIT_WORDS`, `waitWords`, `STOP_WAITING`) and `waitingGates` (now filtering kind `solo`) kept their names.
- `StashAsk` and its reader `parseStashAsk` live in `asked.ts`, not `stash.ts` as notes 3.2 drew it: `asked.ts` reads a stored ask whole, and `stash.ts` posts asks, so the type in `stash.ts` would make the two modules import each other.
- `parseAsked` no longer cuts the list to the newest 20 at load: a stash put holds goods aside, and a cut would lose them. `askPosted` keeps the count.
- The chart stays in `scripts/charts/solo.ts` under the export `soloWait` (renaming the export would edit `scripts/statecharts.ts`, which mythic-4 may also touch); its title is now "An ask waiting for the GM's phone".
- Checks: `bun run test` 6118 pass, 0 fail; `tests/e2e/solo.e2e.ts` 11 passed.

## Step 2: the stash

- `src/core/trade/stash.ts`: `Stash`, `stashRoute`, `stashPut`, `stashTake`, `stashRemove` (here, one write each, logged), `stashAsked` (a player's ask: a put's goods aside in the ask), `stashTaken` (the GM's answer, one write: stash, answer letter, log line), `stashLanded` (the answer on the player's phone), `stashDismissed` and `stranded` (a lost ask put away), `stashDoc`, `stashFits`, `parseStash`, the words.
- `STASH_LINES` (40) and `STASH_REFUSALS` live in protocol.ts beside the doc's reader, as `FEATS_DOC_MAX` does, so protocol.ts does not import stash.ts.
- Measured before trusting the cap (tests/protocol.test.ts, "the worst-case stash doc on the wire"): forty lines of the largest item the game ships (Spellblade, 1,027 characters) are 41,749 characters, 67% of `DOC_MAX` (62,000). Forty lines of a large item the readers take (6,156 characters) are 246,909, four times `DOC_MAX`; ten fit. So `STASH_LINES` stays 40 and a put is also refused `full` when the doc would pass `DOC_MAX` (`stashFits`); the doc is never longer by construction.
- Where the notes did not hold:
  - A put never sent and let go does not give its goods back in the write that let it go: that write is the store's every-write step (store.ts), outside my files. Instead `sent` is raised in the write that puts the letter on its way (sync.ts `flush`), and a lost put that was never sent, or that the room itself refused, shows Take back with no question (`stranded` is false). One sent and lost (or caught by the room gone after a send) asks first. Moving the put back into the store's step is one call (`stashDismissed` for each lost unsent put) in `store.apply`. Built as designed in step 3, below.
  - A stash ask the room took cannot be put away (`TABLES.stash.taken.dismiss` is null; a solo run's can). A take put away would leave the stash for nobody when the GM's phone answers; a put away put is the stash's already.
  - The GM's Remove asks first (`ui.confirm`) instead of a two-second hold under Undo: a hold needs a new kind in `src/core/held.ts`, outside my files. Built as designed in step 3, below.
  - The player's stash is a section under the Bag's Offers tab, not a fourth tab: the tab list is in `src/views/inventory.js` and the API's type in `src/core/api.ts`, outside my files. Moving it is `BAG_TABS` plus one `TradeApi` member. Built as designed in step 3, below.
  - No "As of 21:04." line: the doc's time is not on `SyncApi` (api.ts). Built as designed in step 3, below.
  - Refused words: "The stash is full. Take something out of it first." (the notes' "It holds 40 kinds of item" is false when the size, not the count, fills it); a refused put's second sentence reads as offer.ts's "Back in your bag: 2 Healing Potions."

- Calls the owner might have made otherwise, each with what undoes it:
  - A taken stash ask cannot be put away (above). Undo: set `TABLES.stash.taken.dismiss` to `'end'` in `sync/asked.ts`.
  - A player phone has at most `STASH_WAITING` (10) stash asks waiting; one more is refused `too-many`. Undo: the constant in `stash.ts`.
  - The stash's gold is capped at `GOODS_GOLD_MAX` (1e9); a put past it is refused `full`. Undo: drop the gold term of `stashFits`.
  - The GM's phone logs a refused ask too ("Eldrin asked the stash for 1 Healing Potion: refused (gone)."). Undo: `refusedLog` in `answerOf`.
  - The GM's own hunters put and take with no ask (`stashRoute`), as notes 3.7 said; so does a phone alone.
- Sealed: `stashAsk` and `stashAnswer` reach only the GM and the asking hunter's phone (`sealed: always`), as an offer does.
- The room model (`tests/models/room-world.ts`): the GM's stash starts with Token 21 (`START_STASH`); the players' moves `put` (the bag's first token) and `take` (the stash's first line, read off the GM's phone); the GM's answer `stashOne` through the real `stashTaken`; the answer lands through the real applier and `stashLanded`. The rules (`tradeRule`): no token in two places among the bags, the asides, the puts not yet kept and the stash; a stash line is given at most once. At rest every token, the stash's too, is in one place. The trade walks now see each verdict (`stash given`, `stash kept`, `stash refused`). Model flush raises `sent` as sync.ts does.
- A finding, not fixed: an answer the GM's outbox lets go (a full box) never reaches the player. A `given` answer so lost leaves the stash for nobody; a `kept` one leaves the put waiting, taken, for good. The offer has the same exposure on the GM's Stop.
- What a player sees (390 px, looked at): under the Bag's Offers tab, "Party stash", "The GM's phone answers in the order asks arrive.", "Gold: 0" (Take gold when there is gold), each line with Take, and under "Waiting" each ask ("Take: 1 Healing Potion." / "Waiting for the GM's phone."). The Give window has a "Party stash" chip, the hint "Anyone in the room can take it out." and "Put in the stash". What the GM sees: "Trades and stash" lists "Party stash", "Gold: N", each line with Remove (asks first); its head reads "stash: 1 kind".

## Step 3: the four places built as the design says (second helper on `trade-5`, Nov 22)

The lead widened the file list; each of the four is its own commit.

1. **The Stash tab.** The stash is the Bag's fourth tab (`BAG_TABS` in `src/views/inventory.js`: Items, Offers, Stash, Shop), drawn by the new `TradeApi.stash(box, hid)` (`src/core/api.ts`); the Offers tab holds offers only. The room's `stash` doc redraws the open Stash tab (`sync:docs`). The tab shows on every phone, not only a player's: a phone alone and the GM's own hunters take from the stash too (notes 3.7), and the Stash tab is the one place with Take. Undo: hide the tab button when `app.seat() !== 'player'` in `renderInvBody`. The four tabs keep one line at 360 px (`look.e2e.ts`, "the Bag's four tabs").
2. **A put lost before a try comes back by itself.** `asksKept(save)` (`src/core/trade/stash.ts`) is the store's every-write step for asks, run by `store.apply` right after offer.ts's `lettersKept`, as notes 1.6 and 3.3 draw it: an ask whose letter is on its way is `sent` in that write (`askSent`), and a put whose letter was never sent and is no longer queued or on its way gives its goods back and leaves the list in the write that let it go, whichever writer that was (a full outbox, the room gone). The `sent` mark the first helper put in sync.ts `flush` is gone: the step raises it, in the same write. A put sent and then lost is `stranded`, and its Take back asks first. This now includes a put the room refused after a send, which the first helper let go with no question. Notes 1.6 say why: a first send may have been stored and its answer lost, and the resend then refused (the server answers a resent id with its seq, so a refusal comes from a check before the store, a removal for one). A lost take holds nothing and stays for Put away. The room model's `write` runs `asksKept` after `lettersKept`, as the store does. Tests: tests/stash.test.ts, "a put lost before a try comes back by itself, in the write that lets its letter go", "a put lost after a try is stranded", "a take lost before a try holds nothing".
3. **The GM's Remove is held under Undo.** A new `HeldWhat` kind, `stashRemove` with the line's item id (`src/core/held.ts`, key `stashRemove <id>`), held `GIVE_HOLD_MS` (two seconds) under a toast "You removed Healing Potion × 2 from the stash." with Undo, as the GM's Stop of an offer is; `stashRemove` writes when the toast ends. The question window and its words (`removeBody`) are gone. A second Remove of one line while the first is held is refused by the held machine (one key). Tests: tests/held.test.ts (the kind's key is a write of its own), tests/e2e/stash.e2e.ts "the GM removes a line, held under Undo first" (Undo keeps it; left alone it is gone, logged, and the player's stash reads empty).
4. **"As of 21:04."** `SyncApi.docAt(name)` (`src/core/api.ts`, sync.ts) answers the time the room stored a doc (`Stored.at`, the server's clock, or the GM's own last put), null where `doc` is; a view no longer has a way around it. A player's Stash tab shows "As of 21:04." (this phone's clock, trade.js `clock`) above "The GM's phone answers in the order asks arrive."; a stash on this phone's own save shows no time. The GM's phone publishes the doc only when the stash changes, so the time is when the stash last changed: what is shown was the stash then, and is still the room's last word. The design's "Puts and takes wait for the GM's phone." (`away`) is not shown and its words are gone: a player's phone cannot tell that the GM's phone is away (the room's presence goes to the GM's phone only, sync.ts `presenceOk`), so the line would show always or never. Tests: tests/e2e/stash.e2e.ts (the time the room stored it, in the GM-away test), tests/e2e/look.e2e.ts "a player's Stash tab and the GM's stash list" at 390 px.

## Where the design did not hold (the code wins)

1. **The GM's list is a record, not the log read after `wordsSeen` (4.3).** A log line holds no word id and no hunter key, so the GM could not answer from it. The GM's phone keeps each word it heard in `settings.sync.words` (side `gm`), and "new" is that record's state, not the device pref `wordsSeen`. The log lines are still written, in the same writes.
2. **A word holds its answer (4.4).** The design's lines with `from: 'me' | 'gm'` are one record a word with its answer inside the answered state, so an answer without its word, or an answered word without an answer, cannot be stored.
3. **The answer's window is the view's, opened while the save owes it (4.2 "Applier: once, a land into the thread, and a window").** The applier lands the answer and tells nothing; words.js opens the window on any change of the save while a word is answered and not read, and its close writes read. A page closed before the window showed opens it at the next load.
4. **"Sent." is the machine's landed, not `postNews` (4.4).** `postNews` says only whether the link would send now. The word is landed when the room took its letter, heard in sync.ts's write; a letter refused or let go shows "The room did not take this word." (a lost state the design did not name).
5. **A refused tap is a toast** (`ui.wrote` with `WORD_WORDS.refusals`), not a line in the section.
6. **The section opens with the page only** (as Stories does): a word heard while the GM view is up shows "1 new" on the closed head.
7. **Files outside the brief's list:** `src/edge/sync.ts`, `src/core/api.ts`, `src/app.js`, `scripts/statecharts.ts`, `tests/fixtures/payloads.ts`, `tests/lint.test.ts` (the two lets of words.js named), `docs/guide/machines.md`.

## Calls the owner might have made otherwise

1. **A word takes one answer.** A second (another GM phone's, the same one again) is refused on the GM's phone and lands nothing on the player's. Undo: "Let the GM answer a word more than once" (each word holds a list of answers, the answered cells take another).
2. **A word is seen when the GM opens or closes the section**, so "1 new" goes then. Undo: "A word is new until the GM answers it" (drop `seen` from `GM`).
3. **The notification leaves the word off the lock screen** ("Mira sent you a word." / "Open the GM view to read it."). Undo: "Show the word in the notification" (`notifyBody` becomes the word).
4. **A lost word stays in the thread, with no Send again.** Undo: "Let a player send a lost word again".
5. **The thread shows the words of the room the phone is in;** words of a room left stay in the save until the caps push them out. Undo: "Drop a room's words when the phone leaves it".
