# The lead's look at feature 2 on a screen (Nov 18)

A throwaway Playwright script at 390 by 844 (not committed): a new player through setup, the first gate's window, the Gates view with its personal gate, the odds and their detail, Enter alone and its confirm, the run's window. No console error or warning. The layout holds: the card, its roster, the odds line and detail, both buttons and the confirm fit the width, and the buttons are whole.

Findings, for the next lead to settle before feature 1 (each is small):

1. **A new hunter's first gate can be one it cannot clear.** The run looked at: level 1, the first gate the System opened was a Giant Centipede and a Goblin. The odds read "The party wins 0% of 100 fights", "down in 100%, dead in 43%", and the one run killed the hunter. The numbers are the measured ones (`notes-2-solo.md`, the pregens against two E normals), and the odds say so before the tap. But it is the first thing the System hands a new player, seconds after Hunter created. The lead's pick, not built: the gate the clock opens as it goes from off to on is the even gate (one monster, the hunter's own rank), and the roll by `RULES.solo.pack` and `ranks` starts with the second. A rule of `soloTick`, a case in `tests/solo.test.ts`, a line in the Rules page's solo text.
2. **A player alone who dies has no way back that the screen names.** The window says "The GM's Revive or a resurrection brings you back." A new player with no GM has neither. This is the question in `for-the-owner.md` ("a System resurrection"), and finding 1 makes it pressing: ask the owner now rather than later.
3. **A solo gate's card says "The party wins 0% of 100 fights"** and "The party loses 100%", where the confirm under it says "You clear it in 0% of 100 plays." One hunter is not a party. The odds box takes its words from `oddsLine` and `oddsDetail`; a solo card should read the solo words (`soloConfirmLines` already has them).
4. Under a player's personal gates the view still says "No gates detected. The world is quiet." It means the world's list. Read beside a gate it contradicts it; say "No world gates" there, or leave it out when a personal gate shows.
