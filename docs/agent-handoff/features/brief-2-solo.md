## What a solo run is

- **One drawn play is the run.** The engine of feature 6 (`play.ts`, `policy.ts`, `odds.ts`) plays the hunter (as its row would join a fight: `hunterRow`, its worn statuses, its summoned shadows if it holds any) against the gate's monsters to the end, once, with a seed from the edge. No fight is shown and none is stored: `Save.fight` is untouched. What the play's end says of the hunter's row (HP, mana, fatigue, statuses that stay on a sheet, uses spent, down or dead: `RunEnd`) is written to the sheet through the one writer each already has (the life machine in `vitals.ts`, `sheetEdit`'s totals). The party side won: the gate is cleared and pays. The party side lost: the hunter comes out as the play left it (down is dying with its saves as the life machine has it; dead is dead, and the GM's Revive or a resurrection boon brings it back, as today), and the gate stays. A stalemate (the play's time cap) is a retreat: the cost paid, the gate stays. Name these three outcomes as a union before code.
- **Before entering, the player reads the odds** with the same control the GM has (100 plays, the count shown as it goes): the chance to clear, the likely cost, the chance to come out down or dead. Then Enter asks once ("Enter alone?") with those numbers, and the run is one more play with its own seed. The odds are not the run: say so in the words.
- **One write.** The hunter's totals, the gate's change, the reward (through `grant`, with its window), the log line and any room message are one `store.apply` step in core (a new module beside `gates/pay.ts`; reuse `splitAward`, `xpDue`, `rollLoot`, never a second rule for what a gate pays). A reload between any two parts finds all or none: a test holds it, as `tests/fight-save.test.ts` does for a command.
- **A hunter a fight holds cannot enter** (`fightHold`), nor one who is down, dead, collapsed or Penalized if the rules say a penalty bars gates (read what the penalty does today and say). Refusals are a union with words in core.

## The two kinds of solo gate

- **Personal gates, the System's.** A hunter's own gates, in that hunter's part of the save (weigh: a field of the hunter, or gates of the world list with an owner; pick by which has one writer and travels with the hunter's sheet as the GM's board expects). The System spawns one on a timer: its rank near the hunter's (the spawn weights are `gateSpawn`'s, bounded around the hunter's rank by a rule number), its monsters drawn from the bestiary's built-in seeds of that rank by a rule (`rollGate` and a monsters roll in `gates/spawn.ts`, dice from the edge). The timer's numbers are rules (`RULES.solo`: hours between spawns, how many a hunter may hold, how long one stays before it closes unentered), the time through `day.ts` and a term of `nextWake`, so the one timer wakes for it (AGENTS.md, "A rule of the day's time"). A spawn while the app was closed is made at its next open, once, for the time that passed (at most the cap). The player is told as gate news is told today ("A gate has appeared").
- **World gates the GM marks solo.** A field of the gate (`GateFields`, `newGate`, `fieldsOf`, `parseGate`, the gate form's control, the world doc and its reader in `protocol.ts` with the doc size arithmetic). A player in the room sees Enter alone on it. Its run is played on the player's phone, as a player's dice already are; its result reaches the GM as a room message by AGENTS.md's recipe (`ownSheet`, its payload bounded, its applier on a player's phone `null`): the GM's phone, once by the event id, moves the gate through its machine (`transition`, a Clear paid as `settleClear` pays one hunter) and answers with the award as any award travels (the outbox). Until the GM's phone has answered, the player's phone shows the run as waiting for the room and the gate as entered, never as cleared twice: weigh on paper who holds the truth of that gate between the run and the answer (the player's save holds a pending run by its event id; the world doc is the GM's). Two players entering the same solo gate: the first message the GM's phone takes clears it, the second is refused with words, and its player keeps the cost paid (say this in for-the-owner.md: it is a choice).
- Alone, with no room, a phone is its own GM: personal gates work the same, and its world gates marked solo run and settle in one write on this phone.
- **The GM's switch.** The room's GM can turn personal gates off for the room and set the spawn hours (the room's `build` doc carries the build switches today: weigh that doc against a new one; a player's phone plays by the room's switches as `withRoomBuild` does, never writing them to its save).
