## Steps

1. **The detail component.** One view module (`src/views/detail.js`, `app.api.detail`, registered by the view recipe): given a thing (a status instance, an ability, an item, a zone, a monster's ability, a derived number) it draws the detail: the lines from `describeStatus`, `describeAbility`, `describeItem`, a zone's ops, and for a sheet number its breakdown. The breakdown is a pure function in core beside `derive` (`explain(sheetInputs, key)`: base, stats, gear, statuses, each a labelled amount that sums to the number; a test that every key of `Sheet` sums). It opens in place under the thing through `motion.ts` (`enter`, `dismiss`), one open at a time per list, closed by default, `aria-expanded` on the control. No rule text in the view. Use it on: a status chip (sheet, fight card, the player's HUD), a hotbar ability (press and hold 350 ms opens it without picking; a tap still picks; the timing a token in `motion.ts`), an inventory item, a skill, a zone tapped on the map, a monster's ability on its card, each number of the sheet. Remove the older one-off descriptions it replaces (subtract).
2. **Kits at the table.** By the fight card and map recipes: a stacking status's count on its chip (counting with `numberTo`, a `pop` when it reaches its threshold), uses left on a limited ability, a summon's row and token with its owner and rounds left, a delayed effect's rounds left, an armed reaction on the hotbar (Arm, armed, spent) for a player and on the GM's card, "Surprised" and hidden bodies by 8.7, a forced move animated along its path. A threshold's burst and a reaction firing are earned moments: one `burst` each from the existing kinds, inside the particle budget. Every number comes from `cardOf`/`mapScene`/the doc; the view computes none (the lint holds this).
3. **Typed rolls in the Dice view.** A field that takes Roll20 text (the leading `/r` optional), rolls through `rollFormula` as `notes-dice.md` says, and shows the total with each die: kept plain, dropped and rerolled struck through, exploded marked, successes counted; the last ten rolls kept on this device with a tap to roll again; a bad formula says what is wrong under the field as you type. It is logged and sent to the room like today's rolls with its text. A natural 1 or 20 calls the coin only for a single kept d20 check, by `isCoinRoll`'s rule: say in the notes how a typed roll decides its `kind`.
4. **Tests.** `tests/e2e/battle-map.e2e.ts`, `table-turn.e2e.ts`, `map-extras.e2e.ts`: a player sees a stack count rise and burst, arms a reaction and sees it fire, holds an ability to read it and it is not picked; `tests/e2e/dice` cases for the owner's three formulas with fixed dice; a look case at 360 and 390 for the detail on each kind of thing; `tests/derive.test.ts` for `explain`.
