# Kits of Nikolas and Einar (sheet "ToG - Act 3")

Source: spreadsheet [sheet id removed], tabs "Nikolas" and "Einar". Both read in full (A1:AJ490). Row numbers below are approximate (counted from the returned rows, give or take a few). Quotes are the sheet's own wording, typos kept. Every ability row has: description (col B), Action Cost (K), Energy Cost (L), Times Used (M), Range (N), AoE (O). Energy pools are Health, Stamina, Aura, Mana. "AP" is action points.

## 1. Nikolas

### Concept
A gravity, space and cosmos caster (specialty Power, a Doomed Yuri Space Dragon Sugar Baby) who switches between a stand-still gravity aura and a keep-moving gravity aura, teleports around the field, and turns the lives he ends into constellations that buff the party. His distinct parts are the two opposed growing auras, the portals and teleports, and up to nine orbiting "Baangs" he detonates.

### Trackers (rows 52-80, 97-123, 337-420)
- **Inertial Storage** (self, aura 200ft diameter, class ability and a Condition Table column on enemies and on Nikolas): "Gain Armor & Resist every consecutive turn you use no movement". It also pulls enemies in. It grows every consecutive round active. Reset: any movement (implied by "consecutive"; reset is not written). Cap: none.
- **Momentum's Edge** (self, aura 200ft): "Gain Mystique & Flat Resist Pen every consecutive turn you use all your movement". It damages enemies inside. The tree text says it gives "AP & Flat Resist Pen". Cap: none.
- Anchor (3 rounds, 20 Mana) makes Inertial Storage grow twice as fast. Unbound (3 rounds, 20 Mana) does the same for Momentum's Edge.
- **Paradox of Motion**: once per combat (40 Mana), move all progress from one aura to the other.
- **Baangs** (orbs around him, up to 9; cap raised by Third, Fifth, Seventh and Ninth Body from 1): 1 AP to manifest, or two free a round with A Star Is Born (not acquired). Spent by Detonate (one is unmanifested) or Baang Release (none are lost; the ability is not written out). Supernova auto-detonates them on enemies in Starcaller's Wrath without dismissing them.
- **Starcaller's Wrath** (ground): 30ft column that "remains until all enemies leave its radius". Orbital Laser moves it for 1 AP. Baangs do 1.25x inside it.
- **Constellations / Zodiac** (whole fight, allies' Condition Table columns Endurance, Lost, Primeval): "When ending a life, purify their essence into stardust, using it to create a constellation in your Zodiac. Create one star for free per turn, with all additional stars per turn costing 2 AP to build". Endurance is 3 stars (all allies +15% Health). Lost is 3 stars (+5 Movement). Primeval is 6 stars (+20 Health Regen at three stars; when complete it purges all positive conditions on enemies and ends). Resolve is 5 stars and its text is cut off.
- **Gathering Storm**: a stack condition (a column in the Condition Table and in the Health Table). It is never defined. Astral Storm and Distortion World add to it.
- **Brutalize** (on one enemy, Y/N): healing -50%, armor and resist -25%; he deals 33% extra. It needs the title Might.
- **Rising Moon** (enemy column) is the Eclipse weapon's "Ever Rising Moon".
- **Weak** and **Fragile** are applied by Gravity Surge, depending on which aura is up.
- **Trick** (self, count): +1 each time he deceives someone (Boethian Rhapsody, needs the title Heir of Xūl). Cunning spends one for +1 to a single roll.
- **Domain** (300ft diameter, ground): see Cosmic Creator and Starry Sky.

### Abilities
- Dash: Free, 10 Stamina, self. +10ft movement this round.
- Disengage: Free, 20 Stamina. Leave melee with no opportunity attack.
- Parry: Free, 30 Stamina, melee. Take 0.25x on a success, 0.75x on a fail.
- Transfer: Free, 10 Aura, LOS. An ally acts on his turn.
- React: Free, 30 Aura, LOS. Act before another combatant.
- Inertial Storage and Momentum's Edge: Free, no cost. Both are passive toggles tied to movement. Momentum's Edge is a "Zone" attack class.
- Hover: 3 AP, 30 Aura. Fly up to Movement high, half speed; "Any damage or CC effects knock you out of the air."
- Create Portal: 1 AP, 5 Mana, LOS. Portals link to every portal he has made on the same plane. They last forever.
- Blink: 1 AP, 10 Aura, 60ft, usable as a reaction. Contested Instinct roll. "Can use this to swap with an ally if your Domain is up." One-turn cooldown "unless Domain is active".
- Rendwalk: 3 AP, 20 Aura, 120ft, 30ft. Teleport and leave two rends that pull creatures in and link as portals. Rends last 5 turns. Scales with Power.
- Dimensional Cascade: 5 AP, 40 Mana, LOS, 30ft. "potentially disastrous or meaningless consequences". The damage row has base 8.42; the outcome is undefined.
- Singularity: 5 AP, "All Remaining Mana". It grows exponentially with time.
- Cosmic Creator: Free. One extra Star per turn; once every two turns, displace a person or object inside the Domain.
- Starry Sky: Free, 50 Mana, 300ft. Makes the Domain.
- Manifest Baang: 1 AP, 60ft.
- Starcaller's Wrath: 4 AP, 30 Aura, 1 use.
- Cataclysm: 5 AP, 40 Mana, 100ft. A sun that can be dropped after a turn. Damage scales with "Active Constellations?". While it is up, two free stars a turn.
- Death's Grasp: 2 AP, 30 Aura. Pulls enemies in a 40ft by 20ft rectangle.
- Kneel: 5 AP, 40 Mana, melee. Reducing a target to 0 HP leaves them able only to comply. Granted by Nightfall.
- Gravity Surge (tree): 1 AP. Applies Weak under Inertial Storage, Fragile under Momentum's Edge.
- Earthbound (tree): 2 AP, 30 Aura. Pulls enemies in the air to the ground.
- Tree utilities: Vector Strike (20 Aura, reroll an attack roll once per attack), Proximity Fold (30 Aura, enemies within 60ft count as melee), Zone Stretch (40 Aura, AoE +40ft), Folded Tether (1 AP, 30 Aura), Split Fold (40 Aura), Distortion World (3 AP, 40 Mana, 3 rounds, warping costs -10 Aura, +1 Gathering Storm a round).

### Passives, titles, items
- Weighted Step: advantage to resist knockback. Crush: substitute Power for Strength. Spatial Awareness: cannot be surprised.
- Titles: Godslayer (+5% base stats, +2 rolls), Might (access to Brutalize, +5 base stats), Heir of Xūl (access to Rhapsody, Deception uses Discipline), Defiler.
- Unacquired tree perks of note: Fractured Heavens, Celestial Awakening (10 Mana per star), The Wheel Turns, Equinox, Stellar Meditation, Cosmic Echo.
- Eclipse: "Each successive ability/attack on a single enemy hit in a single turn does 16% more damage".
- Loveblight: every crit multiplies crit damage by 1.5x.
- Fateshaper: Lifebound, an enemy takes a third of the damage it deals to him, for 3 turns.
- Rhapthorne's Scepter has "Sage's Blood" 5/7.
- Others: Stormfront (once per day storm, 300ft), Heartstone Amulet (heal another 2 to 1, once a turn), Agnet of Focus (once per combat +1 AP), Permafrost (a Stack of Cold on a weapon hit).

## 2. Einar

### Concept
A pact-and-boundary controller and archer (the Two Crowned King, specialty Charisma) who places soul-pact zones on the field, then ends them for one-shot payoffs or detonates them all. He also builds a Pain stack economy, a Reveal stack on a bow target, and a Mercy stack for rescues.

### Trackers (rows 71-81, 106-135, 345-440)
- **Boundaries** (on the ground, up to 3, then 4 (Boundary Control), then 5 (Boundary Evocation); AoE sizes 30 to 200ft): placing costs 2 AP (Pact Magic), then 1 AP (Boundary Manipulation). Moving one costs 1 AP. A boundary may be doubled once (2 AP) or made boundless, covering 1000ft (4 AP, one only). Gained from Pacts. Ended Boundaries "are regained after combat". Boundaries detonated by Soul Shatter are lost until a long rest.
- Five boundary types: Hyperbole, Vacuum, Confrontation, Harmony and Ceremony (see below).
- **Stack of Pain** (on self; also on an enemy via transfer): +1 per damage dealt; "+2 Mystique & % Resist Pen". Starts at 3 with Old Wounds. Spent by Fuel the Flesh (2 for 10 Stamina or Aura), Sustained through Suffering (5 for 1 AP), Violence Begets Violence (hands 1 to an attacker as -1 Armor and Resist), Lesson in Pain (3 on an enemy gives Suppress for 1 turn), Truth in Pain (all, at least 10, gives Paralyze 1 turn), All Shall Suffer (all on the field, 1 damage each). Most are unacquired.
- **Reveal** (on one enemy): -0.5% to enemy Discipline per stack, +1 per bow hit (Spirit Archer). Spent by Spirit Shackle (5 for a 1-turn Shackle) and Certain Death (+1 to the raw attack roll per stack).
- **Mercy** (self, permanent): +1 when Eternal Peace kills. Spent by Lamb's Respite (1, twice per combat).
- **Ruthless** (self): from Ceaseless Pursuit kills; spent by Wolf's Hunt.
- **Diplomacy** stack (enemy): from damaging; attack rolls -0.33 per stack. All consumed by Severance.
- **Haste** (self, Limber starts combat with 1): Savage gives +1 on a crit, each grants 1 Movement (2 with Predatory); spent by Cunning (2) and Vicious (5).
- **Stealth** stacks (2, from Shade Step; pass through objects 2 turns).
- **Confrontation** (enemy, per stack: highest base stat -2%; each turn damage = 10x the amount lowered), **Harmony** (ally, Armor & Resist +3 a stack, +1 stack a turn for 5 turns), **Corroded/Dissolved** (enemy), **Stun**, **Shackle**, **Celestial Sea** (ally flag), **Exhaustion** (self, from Hyperbole). Morale (cell K21): a lone value, 5, undefined.

### Abilities
- Dash, Disengage, Parry, Transfer, React: same as Nikolas.
- Silentum Perpetuum: 5 AP, 50 Mana, LOS. Perpetual Silence. "Fail is permanent 10% discipline reduction. Takes 2 turns to cast".
- Soul Shatter: 5 AP, 40 Mana (tree: 50 Mana). Detonates all boundaries; "overlapping stacks damage". Gate: Boundaries stacked.
- Mourning's Light: passive. Learn the greatest regret of anyone, up to Tier 14 or his own tier.
- Shade Step: 1 AP, 20 Aura, 30ft; reaction.
- Eternal Peace: 4 AP, 40 Mana, LOS. "Roll a d20, dealing 1% of the enemy's Max Health times the roll". Kill gives Mercy.
- Lamb's Respite: Free reaction, 100ft diameter. Consumes Mercy. At 1 HP, Invulnerable until the end of their next turn. Works on gods (note).
- Spirit Shackle: Free, 5 Reveal stacks (listed 20 Aura in the tree).
- Diplomacy: Free. +5 to the first Charisma check on a new person.
- Abyssal Whirlpool: 3 AP, 40 Aura, 100ft. Pulls enemies to the centre after a turn. Applies Corroded on entry, Dissolved at the centre. Lasts 2 turns.
- Draconic Avatar: 5 AP, 50 Mana, 60ft, 20ft Surge. Contact applies Dissolved; the Surge ("Surge? Y") dispels the Avatar.
- Shinsuu Reversal: 1 AP, 30 Mana, 60ft reaction. Negates an enemy spell.
- Overcome: 2 AP, 50 Aura. Applies Negate to all enemies in any boundary.
- Catastrophe: 1 AP, 60ft. Applies Ruin; scales with "Turns Within?".
- Pact Redux: Bronze (20 Aura, removes terrain difficulty), Silver (30 Aura, heals allies 1/4 of Missing HP), Gold (40 Aura, dispels negatives), Platinum (50 Aura, Invulnerable 1 turn). All Free and once per combat. Each ends one Boundary.
- Boundaries (1 AP each, 30 Aura): Hyperbole (200ft; downtime in 4 hours, then Exhaustion), Vacuum (60ft; silent), Confrontation (60ft), Harmony (100ft; Stuns enemies when it ends), Ceremony ("Either reduces Stamina and Aura costs or Mana costs for allies inside").
- Bow perks: Soul-Eye (bow attacks target Discipline), Bow Expert (advantage), Bow Master (1 AP), Spectral Shot (30 Aura), Mythical (50 Mana, replaces an attack roll with a nat 20).
- Ceaseless Pursuit: 4 AP, 40 Mana. 5% of the enemy's Missing HP each time he passes a rising DC (5 + 3x).
- Guidance (1 AP, 10 Mana, +5 death save), Echoes of the Fallen (2 AP, 20 Mana).

### Passives, titles, items
- Speaker of the Land: +1 permanent AP. Ring of Mosaic: dead members' souls return to him. Assassination: advantage on all attacks in the surprise round.
- Titles: Guile (access to Diplomacy, +5 Charisma rolls), Guide (+1 Discipline per soul guided), Harbinger (access to Subjugation and Domination), Mythmaker, Godslayer.
- Puzzlebox: "Your first ability per combat costs no energy".
- Pain's Whip Tail: a hit gives Grapple, and melee attacks against them have advantage.
- Crown of Dusk: invisible after 6 seconds undetected within 100ft. The first attack autocrits.
- Ring of Divine Purity: Celestial Sea (once per day, a 100ft pool, 80 (300 w/ bonuses) Health to allies in it). Harmonious Reverberance (once per long rest, nobody except him can attack for a turn).
- Water items each add a Celestial Sea Health bonus (+10 to +80).
- Osteoporosis: three hits fracture a bone, three fractures break it, and a miss does the same to his ribs.
- Orb of Draconic Might (once per day), Fateshaper-like items, Villainous Robes (more Health per life ruined).

## 3. Building blocks
1. A counter on an enemy that abilities add to, other abilities read, and a threshold ability spends (Reveal, Diplomacy, Pain on an enemy, Confrontation, Weak). Einar rows 108-128, 410-420; Nikolas Condition Table rows 98-108.
2. A counter on self gained by a trigger and spent for a bonus or an action (Pain, Haste, Trick, Mercy, Ruthless). Einar rows 395-430; Nikolas row 80-81.
3. A trigger on kill, granting a permanent stack, a star or a constellation (Eternal Peace, Zodiac, Feast of the End-Times). Einar row 56; Nikolas rows 71, 395-410.
4. A trigger on deal-damage, on take-damage and on crit (Pain is Power, Violence Begets Violence, Savage, Loveblight, Permafrost).
5. A boundary/zone placed on the ground with its own rules inside, a cap on how many exist, movable, resizable, and detonated or ended for a payoff (Einar rows 71-81, 345-360). Nikolas has Starcaller's Wrath (row 63), Domain (rows 60-61) and the two auras, which are zones centred on self.
6. A "consecutive turns" growth counter with a reset condition (no movement or all movement): Inertial Storage and Momentum's Edge, rows 52-53, 340-350.
7. A set-of-n collectable (constellation) with partial and complete payoffs and a field-wide effect (Nikolas rows 71-75, 390-415).
8. A reaction ability with a use limit (twice per combat, once per combat, once per day, once per long rest).
9. A status that applies a delayed or periodic effect (Whirlpool pulls after a turn, Cataclysm drops after a round, Lifebound for 3 turns, Anchor and Unbound for 3 rounds, rends for 5 turns).
10. An ability whose cost is "All Remaining Mana", a per-star Mana cost, or "free if first this combat" (Singularity, Celestial Awakening, Puzzlebox).
11. A damage formula that scales with a count or a roll: a percent of the target's max HP times a d20 (Eternal Peace), missing HP, active constellations, Baangs held, stacks consumed.
12. A pact with a condition and a penalty (Silentum Perpetuum: fail is a permanent 10% Discipline loss; Osteoporosis: a missed attack fractures the user's ribs).
13. Cost reduction and bonus-AP effects (Equinox, Distortion World, Bow Master, Agnet of Focus, Fractured Heavens trades stars for AP).
14. Teleport, portal and displacement effects (Blink, Rendwalk, Create Portal, Death's Grasp, Cosmic Creator, Earthbound).
15. A mode lasting a number of rounds that changes the rules (Distortion World, Anchor, Unbound).
16. A title or item that gates access to an ability (Might gives Brutalize, Heir of Xūl gives Rhapsody, Guile gives Diplomacy).
17. A weapon-type rule (Bow attacks target Discipline; a bow hit gives a Reveal stack).
18. Contested rolls and rerolls (Blink, Vector Strike, Mythical).
