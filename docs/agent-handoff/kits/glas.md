# Glas (the lead's summary from a reader's report, Nov 14)

A blood and time caster whose kit builds counters on each enemy and cashes them in, and who pays for power with his own health.

- Per enemy: **Sanguine Resonance** ("Each instance of damage imparts a Stack... heals you for a portion of all damage done to an opponent. When the amount of stacks exceeds the opponents Vitality stat, it executes"), **Celestial Rot** ("Each instance of damage imparts a Stack of Celestial Rot. Celstial Rot reduces the opponents Vitality and inflicts damage every turn"), **Vision** ("Every 2 stacks of Vision lowers the roll necessary to crit against an enemy by 1. Critting resets the stacks").
- Stack tools: Sine Sanguis (free, 20 Aura: doubles all stacks he imparts on an opponent), Descecrate (doubles the stacks one opponent in his domain takes from him this turn), Fester (damage by the target's Resonance stacks, adds one), Blood Rift (a stack of Vision), Eye of Carnage (8 Vision to all enemies in 40 ft; advantage on defending rolls against those hit for 3 turns), Children of the Grave (1 Vision per damage instance; "If an opponent dies while cursed, you can raise their ghost to do your bidding for 2 turns").
- Payoffs: Wrath of the Aspect (by "the total number of Celestial Rot Stacks on the field"), Tides of Blood ("Sacrifice a percentage of your Max HP per turn in exchange for damage based off of Sanguine Resonance Stacks... The longer you channel the more damage"), Hemoplague (5 AP, 30 Mana: stats per enemy hit and heal 15% of max HP; "After 3 turns, all enemies take massive damage and gain X Stacks of Celestial Rot per enemy hit").
- Health as a cost: Crimson Pact ("Sacrifice half of Max Health to gain stats to all except Vitality"), Minor Ascension (drain "based off your missing HP from all enemies in the area, remains in effect until a full turn passes with no enemies in range"), a title giving Vamp per percent of missing HP.
- Drain: Transfusion, Sanguine Siphon. Modes: Everbleed (2 turns, abilities auto-hit, minimum 1 damage), Carnage tiers (not defined in the sheet).
- Time (the owner: "a little fucky"; the rewind is not wanted for now): Chronobreak (back to "your exact status 3 turns ago" and an area blast), Second Impact ("enemies take an additional 33% of the damage you done to them this turn"), Recursion (retry an action up to 3 times), Temporal Flux (advantage for a turn), Continuum Shift (untargetable this and next turn, +5 movement, a shockwave on return), Expected Outcome (a d20 rolled at session start that can be added to any roll).
- Growth: Consume ("each consumed person permanently increases stats. Stats are no longer gained by levels").
- Items: stats from enemies killed; heal 15% of a kill's max HP; 5 attacks on one enemy gives penetration for 3 turns; store damage taken and pay it out over 3 turns; mark an enemy that must be killed within the day.
- Undefined in the sheet: the Carnage tiers, the stat bonuses of Hemoplague and Crimson Pact, how much Resonance heals, most damage numbers, the skill trees.
