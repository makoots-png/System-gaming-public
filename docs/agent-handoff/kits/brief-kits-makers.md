## Steps

1. **Unit 15, parsers and packs**: as design 5.3 (`worn` on a skill and an item, a monster's starting stacks, packs), each through its one parser with its bounds, cases in the parsers' tests and `tests/pack.test.ts`.
2. **The status maker** (`forge.js` with `maker.js`'s effects editor): a collapsed group per block, each closed by default and showing one line when set: Stacks (cap, how they leave, per-stack scaling), At a count (the threshold: its number or formula, what lands, spent or kept), When (triggers: the event from the closed list with its tag filter, the limit, what lands and on whom), When it ends (the delayed effect; what a cleanse does), Aura and Spread as today, and wave 2's ops by the op-kind recipe (the round-trip test asks for every member of `OP_KIND_SET`). The preview's sentences come from `describeStatus` and must read as rules a player understands ("Each hit adds a stack, up to 10. At 10: 3d6 damage, and the stacks are spent.").
3. **The ability form** (`maker.js` `ability`, used by `skills.js` and the monster maker): Costs (HP flat or a share, stacks of a status on self or target), Needs (a mode, a count, uses a fight or a rest), the scalers of 8.0 as ticks of the formula field (each with its suggested start and scale from `worth.ts`, both editable, Reset to suggested), a delayed hit, a forced move, a reaction (armed, what it answers), an edge, a summon, a zone act; the new formula names offered as chips that insert text (`target.stacks(…)`, `missing`, `field(…)`). Roll20 dice notation works in the formula field with its error on the row. The preview shows the ability's sentences and its worth against its kind's bound from `worthOf`: a plain bar and a line ("12% over a strike's budget at C": a warning, never a refusal).
4. **Items and monsters**: an item's `worn` status and trigger (item maker), a monster's starting stacks and reactions (monster maker).
5. **The built-ins a GM can open and copy** (design 5.2 and 8.12): at least a stacking status with a threshold, a trigger passive, an ability with an HP cost and a scaler, a delayed curse, a mark and its detonation, a reaction, a summon, listed in the Workshop with Duplicate. Each inside its budget (the content tests check).
6. **Prove it by building the kits.** In `tests/e2e/makers.e2e.ts` (or a new `kits.e2e.ts` if it grows past the file's shape), through the UI only: a GM builds Resonance (stacks, heal on hit, burst at the bearer's Vitality), Fester (damage by the target's stacks, adds one), Hemoplague (delayed damage after 3 rounds), Crimson Pact (a mode for half max HP) and an execute-style scaler; gives them to a hunter; a fight is played on two phones and the test reads the stacks on the foe's chip, the burst, the delayed hit landing and the HP paid, from the fight and the room doc. Then the same GM shares the kit as a pack code and another GM imports it. Every op kind and every new field round-trips (the existing round-trip test, extended).
7. **Close.** deps, charts, typecheck, the whole `bun run test`, browser files `makers`, `kits` if made, `customization`, `custom-skills`, `sharing`, `look` (a look case: each new group at 360 and 390 has no horizontal scroll and 44 px targets). `scripts/ui-shots.ts` steps for the new groups; look at the shots yourself and fix what is cramped.
