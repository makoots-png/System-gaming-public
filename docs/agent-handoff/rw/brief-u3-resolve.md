3. **Statuses in ticks.** In `effect-ops.ts`: applying a status (one instance per key, stronger wins, as today), its duration with potency, tenacity and the boss and elite factors (design 3.1 rule 18 and 4.2), expiry by time, pro rata settling of a per-round op at a turn start (design 1.3), and `modified(sheet, statuses)` which applies `stat`, `speed`, `root` and the like to a `Sheet` through `derive` (one exhaustive switch over `Op`, so a new op kind fails the typecheck until it has its cell; if unit 2 left a seam for modifiers, use it, otherwise add the smallest one to `derive.ts` and say so). Commit.
4. **`resolve.ts`.** `needs(ability, user, bodies, aim, grid, rules)` (which dice and which reaction rolls the edge must roll) and `resolve(ability, user, bodies, aim, grid, rolls, now, rules)` returning either a refusal with a reason from a closed union, or the outcomes and the changed bodies (the user's cost, cooldown and `at`; each target's place, HP, shield, stagger, threat, channel, statuses) by design 3.1 rules 2 to 19 in that order, each rule a small named function a reader can find by its rule number in a comment-free way (name them `taunted`, `onCooldown`, `payCost`, `inRange`, `landTime`, `whereItEnds`, and so on). No dice are rolled here and nothing is validated here: the rolls come in, and the types are trusted. A missing roll is a refusal (`needsRoll`), not a throw. The channel's own machine (start, grow, release, break) is the next unit's; here a hit reports `channelBroken` by rule 17. `tests/resolve.test.ts`: the three worked examples of design 3.5 with their numbers typed in from the paper script's output, one test per rule that breaks when that rule is removed (range, taunt, cooldown, cost with `costCut`, a rooted target, the ring's share, a resisted direct use dealing half with no status, crit from the target's low faces, penetration percent then flat, the floor of 1, the shield absorbing first with larger-holds, threat only on a monster, the weak-hit stagger rate, the channel-break threshold, a boss's shorter control), a refused use changing nothing (deep equality of the input bodies), and properties over a seeded generator: HP never leaves 0 to max, a higher armor never raises damage, a higher reaction roll never moves a target from out to caught, `resolve` never mutates its inputs. Commit.
5. **Check and notes.** `bun run typecheck`, `bun run test`, `bun run deps -- --write` and commit what it changes. `docs/agent-handoff/rw/notes-u3.md`: exported names with one line each, each place the design was silent or contradicted itself and what you chose, which principle changed a choice, the exact last line of `bun run test`.
