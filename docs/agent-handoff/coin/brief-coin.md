## Why (the owner's goal)

the owner is the GM of a tabletop game played on phones. He has a table rule: "It's heads or tails on a 1 or a 20. It should inform me of the players decision (heads or tails) and I pick if they were correct or not (this is hidden to the player) unless it's combat. In combat it's just the true roll 1 or 20." The moment a player rolls a natural 1 or 20 is the best moment at his table, and he wants to hold it in his hand without the player knowing whether he did. He also asked that everything feel satisfying and be architecturally sound: one explicit machine, typed messages, nothing implicit.
