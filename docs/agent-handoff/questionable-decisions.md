Every call a lead made that he might have made otherwise, newest last. Each says what was decided, why, and the one thing to tell a lead to undo it. The same list is the Google Doc "Hunter System: questionable decisions" (id `1Bg6o4g2n_CtvoxHB9ey0tgdYoyN8H-29P_n95yo2GWg`; add a row there with the Docs connector, read it with Drive `read_file_content` when he says he answered in it); a lead adds a row to both in the turn it decides.

## Decided while he was away (Nov 22, night)

1. **A landed stun always costs its target a full round.** Before, a one-round stun only stopped the target acting until one round from now, so it delayed a boss by about half a round and sometimes by nothing; a party's control took 6 to 8% of a boss's turns against a target of 10 to 35%. Now a hold adds its length to the target's next turn. It also makes a monster's stun on a hunter cost a full turn. Undo: "a stun is a floor again". Status: to build, in the balance unit after the mythic fix, with every simulation row measured again.
2. **A consumable used in a fight costs half a standard action.** His words: "Not a full turn, but should cost something". Undo: give another share; it is one rule, `fight.consumableTime`.
3. **A rest clears 75% of current fatigue.** His word was "largely". Undo: give another share; it is one rule, `rest.fatigue`.
4. **A dungeon is a gate, for "two rests per dungeon".** The count is per hunter per gate and starts again at the next gate. Undo: say what else a dungeon is (a session, a day).
5. **The dice roller gets no difficulty table.** Its starting number to beat is one number for every rank; he sets the number for the thing being tried. Undo: "build difficulty presets" (Easy 10, Hard 15, Heroic 20, or his own).
19. **A solo run's end, won, lost or retreated, gives a hunter its two rests back.** A solo run is a gate of its own, so its end starts the count again; only a win gives HP, mana and fatigue back. Undo: "a lost run keeps the rests spent".
20. **A dead hunter's rest count also starts again at a Clear.** It gets no HP, mana or uses back (his rule), but the count belongs to the gate, so the next gate starts with two. Undo: "a dead hunter keeps its rests spent".
21. **The keystones design is accepted as written** (`features/notes-11-keystones.md`, design A): a stat holding 30% of the points a hunter spent earns a Mark at rank D, the two highest stats at 25% each a Confluence at rank C, a stat over 55% an Apex at rank B; 44 powers; earned by the points with no tap; gear never counts; elites, bosses and mythics earn them too, shadows never. This is how "specialising must pay" is built. Undo: "no keystones", or name the part to change (the shares, the ranks, monsters holding them).
22. **The dice roller starts every hunter on 13 to beat** (the old E rank "Standard"). His ruling: the number is the same for every rank, and he sets it for the thing tried. Undo: give the starting number.
23. **A gate kept open because a party was in it when the day passed its break day does not break when the party leaves.** His words covered only "it doesn't break if someone is in it". Undo: "it breaks when they leave".
24. **Stealth beats sight at the same level, and every built-in hide is one level lower** (Stealth and Invisible are 0, 0, 1, 2, 3, 4 by rank), so each hides from the same sights as before and no balance number moved. Undo: "keep the old levels" (hides then get one step stronger).
25. **Each job leans to one pair of stats, the lead's choice**: Fighter Strength and Vitality, Assassin Agility and Insight, Ranger Agility and Control, Mage Power and Control, Tanker Vitality and Discipline, Healer Discipline and Presence, Necromancer Presence and Power. A job's pair is the hunter's Confluence when both stats hold a quarter of the points. Undo: name the pair for a job.
26. **The mythic encounter design is accepted as written** (`features/notes-11-keystones.md`, design B): a mythic fights in phases from one row of data; its HP stops at each phase's gate, so no burst skips a phase; it announces a big move a turn ahead and the party's answer (a stun, a broken stagger bar, enough damage, stepping out, a shield) cancels or weakens it; a phase past its clock is enraged and the fight is lost. Its 15 calls, each with the words that undo it, are the last section of that file. It ships switched off until its simulation rows hold 5 to 10%. Undo: "no mythic phases", or name the call.
27. **A monster's body sets its HP and power, at every kind**: a skirmisher has 1.7 times a brute's HP, a caster 1.8 times its HP and three quarters of its power (a caster's curse is never dodged). This is what makes a mythic of any body a 5 to 10% fight (measured 6.8%, 7.0%, 7.8%). It also makes every skirmisher and caster monster tougher than before: such bosses at S last about 8 rounds against a brute boss's 7.3. Undo: "only mythics get the body's HP", or give the rounds a boss should last.
28. **A mythic cannot be extracted as a shadow** (being built in `mythic-1`). The balance plan's default; before, a mythic had a normal monster's extraction number. Undo: "mythics can be extracted", with how hard it should be.
29. **A mythic's warning never aims at a hunter it cannot see or cannot target** (a hidden hunter, or one made untargetable). It picks among the others. Undo: "a mythic's omen can aim at a hidden hunter"; it is one filter in `omenAim` (`src/core/combat/mythic.ts`).
30. **A mythic's warning with no hunter left to aim at falls on the mythic's own tile**, so it still goes off and the fight never stalls on it. Undo: "an omen with no one to aim at is dropped"; it is the last two lines of `omenAim`.
31. **A keystone is worth about five times a title** (being built in `keystones-2`). The design pegged a keystone's strength to a title of its rank (2, 3 and 4 points), which cut the big ones to almost nothing: Worldbreaker came out as 3% more on a strike. The budget goes to about 10, 15 and 20 points so the keystones keep the amounts they were designed with, and the fight simulation's bounds (unit K5) are what hold them. Undo: "a keystone is worth a title"; it is one rule, `fight.keystone.budget`.
32. **The top keystones (apexes) are budgeted at 25 points, marks at 10, the two-stat ones at 15, and nine keystones were reshaped to fit.** Worldbreaker no longer adds damage: it fills half of a foe's stagger bar. Tactician works on a hit. Each changed row says why in `src/core/registry/keystones.ts`. Undo: name the keystone and what it should do; the amounts are checked again by the fight simulation (unit K5).
33. **Blasts and curses both count as a "spell"** for a keystone or status that triggers on a kind of ability, beside their own kind. Undo: "no spell tag"; it is `SPELL_KINDS` in `src/core/registry/ability-kinds.ts`.
34. **On the GM's Party board the hidden dice weight is called "Weight"; in a fight it is called "Fudge".** Undo: "call it Fudge everywhere" (the words in `src/views/gm.js` `weightTable`, `weightCard`).
35. **A mythic held at a phase's HP line takes no damage below it until its next phase starts**, a stagger effect fills half as much of a mythic's bar, and the statuses a phase gives a mythic can be removed like any other. Undo each by name: `heldAt` in `row-life.ts`, `staggerTaken` in the rules, the `worn` line in `monsterRows`.
36. **A forced next roll (20 or 1) is used up by the hunter's next d20 roll that shows that face.** The GM's phone cannot see which roll a player's phone made with the force, so it counts the first roll showing the forced face. One case in twenty is off: a roll made just before the force arrived that lands on that face by luck uses the force up. A forced face changes only d20s; the plus or minus weight moves every die. A hunter who joins late is sent its weight on joining. Undo: say what should use a force up instead.
37. **Keystones stay switched off at tables until their fight balance is measured and their screens are built** (units K5 and K6), then they go on for everyone. Without this, hunters, elites and bosses would have started wearing keystones in live fights with nothing on screen saying so. Undo: "turn keystones on now"; it is one rule, `fight.keystone.on`. A hunter also now stores which job's Job Change it finished, so deleting the finished quest no longer loses its awakened powers.
38. **A mythic's warning aimed at itself or at everyone follows the mythic**, so shoving the mythic one tile does not make the warning miss. And when the GM drops a warning, the mythic still takes a normal turn. Undo: "an omen stays on the tile it was called on" (`omenAim`'s two `me.id`), "a dropped omen costs the mythic its turn". Mythic phases stay switched off at tables until the screens for them are built (unit MY7), not when the numbers are done (MY5), because without the screens a GM would be stuck on the turn a warning lands.
39. **The party stash is a tab of the Bag on every phone**, a phone playing alone included (there it is that phone's own stash). It holds at most 40 kinds of item and a billion gold; a phone can have 10 stash requests waiting at once; a put the room refused after it was sent waits for the player to take it back rather than returning by itself; the GM's Remove can be undone for two seconds. Undo each by name: "the Stash tab only shows in a room", `STASH_LINES`, `STASH_WAITING`, "a refused put comes straight back".
40. **Answering a mythic's warning will push back the clock of its phase** (to build, unit MY5c). Measured: a party that ignores every warning still beats a mythic 5 to 8% of the time, the same as one that answers them, because nearly every fight runs into the phase's clock and the mythic's enraged turns decide it either way. So an answered warning buys the party time on the clock, and ignoring them does not. Undo: "answers do not move the clock". Also moved to make the fights hold their bounds: the Fourth Rider's book opens at 55% HP, not 70%; Zaratan's Sea Rises aims at the weakest hunter and its Dive lasts 4 rounds; a taunt no longer cancels Zaratan's Trench (a hold or a root does).
41. **Every title now has a rank band and a point budget (E 1, D 2, C 3, B 3, A 4, S 5), and a title that spends more is told so, never refused.** Three titles gave max HP and were far over any band (+20 max HP is worth 33 points at E), so they give Vitality now: Still Standing +1, Marathoner +3, One Who Overcame Adversity +2 (was +20 max HP). On this scale a title's once-a-fight heal of 20 HP is over even at S; one of about 8 HP fits rank C. Undo: "titles give max HP again", or give other budgets; they are one rule, `titleBudget`.
42. **A mythic's warning will be answered only by a turn the party chooses, not by the hits it would land anyway** (to build, unit MY5d). Measured after decision 40 was built: a party that ignores every warning still wins 3 to 8% (the aim is under 1%), because its ordinary hits break the bar or deal the burst a warning asks for as often as a party that tries to. So a stagger or burst answer will be sized past what ordinary play lands in the warning's window, and a party meets it by holding its strongest use for the warning: spend now or save for the omen is the decision. Undo: "ordinary hits answer a warning" (the rule `fight.mythic.answerReach` at 0).
43. **Some of a mythic's warnings will ask for a turn that is not an attack** (to build, unit MY5e): guard or shield the hunter it is aimed at, or step off the tile it is aimed at. Decision 42 was built and measured: counting only what lands after the warning cut the wins of a party that ignores warnings to 2 to 4% (from 3 to 8%; the aim is under 1%), but breaking the bar and dealing the burst are still the turn a party plays anyway, so skipping those two answers costs nothing. The mixed party has nothing it can hold for a warning without losing the race against the clock. Undo: "every warning is answered by hitting it".
44. **The System's raise is offered only after the fight ends, and it does not wait for the GM's approval** (being built, unit rulings-21). His ruling was "System raise" with no GM button; the rule that every System message waits for the GM would have put a button back, and a dead row inside a fight is the GM's to revive. Undo with: "the System's raise waits for the GM" or "offer the raise during the fight".
45. **A shield from a trigger is now priced as the same HP healed, and the keystone Second Wind went from 25% to 35%.** Before, a trigger's shield cost nothing in the budget, so the new Lifeline Locket (Rare+, neck: once a fight under 30% HP, a shield of 10% of max HP) could not be priced. With the price, Second Wind spent 7.3 points against a band of about 10, so it was raised to fill it (10.2). Undo with: "Second Wind back to 25%".
46. **Thornmail's healing cut is still left out.** Priced as the mirror of heal power, a 40% cut costs 47.8 points and Thornmail has 0.59 left, which buys a 0.5% cut. It comes back when the simulation measures how much a foe heals, so the cut has a real worth. Last Light needed no change: no title the game ships heals, and the 8 HP example already fits its band. Undo with: "give Thornmail the healing cut anyway, at 40%".
47. **A raise on debt stores what the hunter owes (10 Clears at its rank: 1,000 gold at E), and nothing collects it yet.** The bag shows the debt. The number is a rule (`reviveDebtClears`) the GM's room rules can change. Paying it off is its own unit, not built. Undo with: "store no debt, work it out from the ladder" or "use the same number as the revive cost".
48. **The System's raise is offered only on a player's phone, never in GM mode, and it costs levels like any other raising** (2 levels in the test's case). Undo with: "offer the raise in GM mode too" or "a System raise costs no levels".
49. **No mythic warning is answered by hitting the mythic any more.** Each warning is answered by stepping off its tile, shielding the hunter it is aimed at, or stunning the mythic (unit MY5e, built, switched off). Decision 43 said "some"; two passes had measured that a hit is the turn a party plays anyway, so a hit answer never told a party that answers from one that ignores. With this, a party that ignores every warning wins under 1% (0.000, 0.002, 0.008 for the three mythics) and a party of 3 that answers wins 5.8 to 6.8%. An answer now buys 3 rounds, was 1, and the phase clocks are shorter. Undo with: "every warning is answered by hitting it" or "answers buy one round".
50. **Each mythic's warnings were rewritten to fit decision 49.** The Final Note is made smaller by a stun, not by breaking its bar; Weighed and Named are cancelled by a shield only; Stars Fall comes after Weighed, aims at the farthest hunter and hits at 1.0; Sinking hits 1.0 and Trench 1.2; a hunter steps out of an area only when the hit would drop it; HP factors Angel 1.0, Rider 0.78. Still out of bound: Zaratan's stun-answered warning comes in its last phase and is met 0.09 times a fight, and three cannons still beat a brute mythic 19 to 66% (unit mythic-balance, started). Undo with: "break its bar to make the note smaller", or name the warning to put back.

## Decided earlier in this session (Nov 21 to 22), told to him in chat

6. **"25% harder" auto runs: HP and damage each up by the square root of 1.25 (about 11.8%).** A fight's difficulty is read as the foes' HP times the damage they deal. A same-rank hunter's win chance on a same-rank personal gate went E 45 to 37%, C 43 to 35%, S 59 to 50%. Undo: give the win chance he wants; it is one rule, `solo.autoHarder`.
7. **A monster picks its target by damage thrown at it before its own armor or resist.** So a boss's armor no longer turns it from the tank to the caster. Undo: `fight.threatBeforeDefence` to 0.
8. **With death saves gone, the target "a hunter goes down in at most 1 boss fight in 4" was dropped.** Down and dead are one thing now. What stands: a death in under 1 boss fight in 20 (measured 0 at C, about 1% at S). Boss fights may now be too safe. Undo: give the share of boss fights in which he wants a hunter to die.
9. **A hunter revived in a fight stands at 1 HP with no statuses.** Undo: give the HP (a share of max).
10. **The feat On Your Feet was removed.** Nothing brings an ally up in a fight any more. Undo: say what it should count (revives by a crystal he hands over, for one).
11. **A coin he rules in a room records nothing in the player's Chronicle.** Recording "called it right" would tell the player the outcome. Undo: "record it when I reveal it".
12. **The fudge factor's defaults.** His own tray roll outside a fight is not weighted. The weight set outside a fight and a fight's weight do not carry into each other. The shadow extraction's check is not weighted. +2 means 2 faces on every die, a d4's the same as a d20's. On damage dice the weight moves each die's face, never the total, so every face shown is one the die can roll. Two forced rolls that ask opposite faces of one die cancel. Undo: name the one to change.
13. **Example items are far weaker than the plan's amounts.** The plan's Bulwark shield (10 + 20% of max HP every 3 rounds) spent five times a Rare+ item's budget; the built one is 10 + 5% every 8 rounds. Undo: "let example items break the budget", or raise the budget for items with actives.
14. **The Lifeline item and Thornmail's healing cut are held back.** The first needs an "under 30% HP" trigger that is not built; the second cannot be priced yet. Both return when those exist.
15. **Percent magic penetration is judged against a boss with high resist, not by raising every boss's resist.** Raising it would make magic worse against all bosses. Undo: "bosses should all resist magic more".
16. **The mythic's numbers.** Twice a normal body's armor, resist and power, 5.2 bodies of HP, 5 times the XP. Fitted to every body since row 27.
17. **A save with a dying hunter in it loads that hunter up at full HP.** Old saves need not work (his rule), so nothing was built to turn them dead.
18. **A dead ally can still be picked as the target of an ally ability, which then does nothing.** Scored 3, under his rule of 6, so not built.
