## 1. First 10 seconds (first-load screen only)

I see a dark sci-fi screen called "SYSTEM" with a popup: "[SYSTEM] You have been chosen as a Player. Accept?" Behind it is a dimmed dashboard for someone called "Sung Jin-Woo, Wolf Slayer" with HP, Mana and stats.

- **What I think it is:** some kind of RPG video game, or maybe someone else's saved game I've been dropped into. I can't tell which.
- **Who it's for:** people who already know what this is. Not me.
- **What I do first:** click "Yes", because it's the only bright button and "No" is crossed out. I don't know what I'm accepting. The crossed-out "No" reads as either a bug or a joke I'm not in on.

## 2. After clicking around

**What I figured out, mostly from the Rules tab and the "?" popup:** this isn't a game that plays itself. It's a character sheet, dice roller, monster list and fight tracker for a tabletop role-playing game. One person is the "GM" (never spelled out), and players join from their phones with a room code. Names like Igris, Beru and Sung Jin-Woo make me suspect it's based on an existing story, but the site never says.

**Still confused:**

- **Welcome popups.** "The System" is never explained, "GM" is never defined, and the sample character vanishes after I click Skip. I became a blank "Hunter" and wondered if I'd lost something.
- **Am I allowed to do this?** On Status I can give myself Bleeding, heal to full, add skills, and create a Mythic item. In Combat and Bestiary I can add an "S boss" to a fight as a player. I can't tell if this is an honour system or the GM's job.
- **The PLAYER pill.** It looks like a label, but clicking it offers "Switch to GM mode?" with no password. So anyone can be GM?
- **Attributes.** Every stat is 10 with "+2" under it, and I don't know why. "Points can be undone with − until you confirm", but I see no Confirm button. "Tap a stat to roll a check" doesn't say what a check is.
- **E badge.** There's an "E" next to the XP bar. I learned much later that it's a rank.
- **Build and job.** Build offers six paths (Vanguard, Striker and so on) with only stat abbreviations and no description. I can't tell if the choice is permanent. Onboarding asked for a "Starting job", and I don't know if that's the same thing.
- **Derived.** It's a wall of numbers: Speed 100 (of what?), Reaction, Will, "Rank bonus", then a "Check bonus" grid repeating the same +2 eight times.
- **HP and fatigue controls.** The HP box says "7 or +5", which means nothing to me. Fatigue has "Rest", "Full", "Short rest" and "Long rest". I can't tell if "Full" means full fatigue or fully rested.
- **Bag.** There's a "Shop" tab and a "Store" button, and I can't tell them apart.
- **Sub-tabs.** "Stats / Build / Skills / Titles / Gear / Bag" look like tabs, but everything is on one giant page and none is highlighted.
- **Quests.** A red "⚠ Daily quest incomplete. Penalty in 06:11" appears before I've done anything. I don't know which quest or what penalty, or whether it's a real-world timer. The page opens on "Active 0" while the quest it's warning about hides under "Daily 1".
- **Gates.** "No gates detected. The world is quiet." I don't know what a gate is or what I'm supposed to do about it.
- **Combat.** "Add party hunters, monsters or shadows to begin" mentions shadows, but there's no way to add one. "Quick line (NAME X3, RANK, ARCHETYPE)" is jargon. I see no Start button.
- **Bestiary.**
  - "Extractable" is on every card and never explained.
  - "Shot · Shot, quick · On a foe, 12 tiles" repeats itself.
  - Units flip between "tiles" and "ft".
  - Stone Golem says "speed 20 ft" next to SPEED 100.
  - "Ignore 2 AC" appears, but there's no AC anywhere else.
  - "Penalty Zone resident" means nothing to me.
- **Dice.** "/r 2d20kh1 + 4", "Adv / Dis" and "E Standard (DC 13)" are all unexplained. A floating "d20" button follows me everywhere with no label.
- **Rules.** It opens with a "GM quick card" and abbreviations like "comm / unco / lege / myth". Then come formulas like "Effective points = 10 × (score/10)^0.8". One line says "a score of 14 adds about 2", and the worked example right below says Strength 14 gives +1.
- **Room, Join and Data.** The Join pill, the Room tab and "Campaign room → Open room" on Data are three doors to one thing, and none says what a room is. Data adds "GM key", "campaign", ".JSON", "HS1…." codes and "bundles and maps".
- **The "?" guide.** It mentions a "Workshop", a "GM tab" and "the room button at the top". None of those labels exist on my screen.

## 3. What's missing

- **What this is.** One plain sentence would do: what it is, that you need a group and a GM, and what it's based on.
- **A first step.** After "Begin" I land on a 2,700px sheet with no "do this next" and no confirmation that anything happened.
- **Role boundaries.** Nothing says what a player does versus what the GM does.
- **A glossary or tooltips.** I needed them for rank, check, DC, gate, shadow, extractable, title, fatigue and penalty.
- **Useful empty states.** They're flavour text ("The world is quiet") instead of saying who fills them and how.
- **Feedback.** In what I saw, there's nothing after onboarding, and the Join button is greyed out with no reason given.
- **Trust.** "Saved in this browser" is good. But nothing says what a room sends, where it goes, or who can see my character. There's no about page, author or contact.
- **Inviting friends.** "Create room as GM" doesn't say what happens next or what I send people.
- **Solo use.** I can't tell if I can do anything alone.

## 4. Where I'd close the tab

1. **First popup.** "You have been chosen" with a crossed-out No feels like a prank or a scam. Half of strangers leave here.
2. **Landing on Status after Skip.** I count about 60 buttons, with no goal and nothing happening.
3. **The red penalty countdown.** I'm being threatened by a thing I don't understand.
4. **Gates and Combat as a player.** They're empty, and I have nobody to play with.
5. **Rules.** I went looking for an explanation and got exponent formulas.

## 5. Mobile vs desktop

- **Bottom bar.** In the screenshots it sits across the content and hides controls: the Add status row, the "Add monsters" button, three of the Quick check buttons on Dice, and the "Sound on" checkbox. That may be a capture quirk, so check it on a real phone. If it's real, it's broken.
- **Page length.** Status is about 14,000px, Bestiary about 29,000px and Rules about 34,000px. That's unusable scrolling.
- **"More" menu.** Rules and Room, the two things a newcomer needs, are buried there. On those pages "More" is highlighted, so I can't tell where I am.
- **Lost name.** The word "SYSTEM" disappears from the header, leaving just a "!" icon.
- **Clipped sub-tabs.** The row cuts off at "Gear" with no hint that it scrolls.
- **Clipped guide.** The "How it works" popup has its title cut off at the top.
- **Nav order.** GM mode reorders the bottom bar, so Combat and Quests swap places.
- **Desktop wording.** Desktop has the reverse problem: it says "this phone only", "Tap" and "Vibrate".

## 6. Prioritized fixes

1. **Critical: nothing says what this is.** A stranger can't tell game from tool. Put one sentence on the first screen: "A companion app for a tabletop RPG based on X. One GM runs the game, and players track their hunter here." Add a permanent About link.
2. **Critical: the welcome popup is an in-joke.** "Chosen as a Player" and the struck-through No confuse anyone outside the fandom. Keep the flavour as a subtitle and add a plain line under it. Make "No" either work or go away.
3. **Critical: "Who are you?" assumes I know the roles.** Describe each: "Player: I have a character and someone else runs the game" and "Game Master (GM): I run the game for others". Add "Just looking around".
4. **Critical: no first step after onboarding.** Show a three-item checklist: spend your 12 points, pick a path, join your GM's room or roll a first check. Confirm "Hunter created".
5. **Critical if real: the mobile bottom bar covers controls.** Verify on a phone. Pin it to the true bottom and pad the page so nothing sits under it.
6. **High: the penalty countdown ambushes newcomers.** Don't start it until onboarding is done. Say what the quest and the penalty are, and make the banner link to the Daily tab.
7. **High: players see GM controls with no guidance.** Hide or label GM actions in player mode: spawning monsters, free edits, adding statuses. Or state "your table runs on trust, and the GM usually does this".
8. **High: the PLAYER pill is a hidden mode switch.** Label it "Mode: Player ▾". Explain what GM mode is for, and spell out "Game Master" once.
9. **High: the Status page is overwhelming.** Make the sub-tabs real tabs and show vitals and attributes by default. Collapse Derived and Check bonus behind "Details".
10. **High: attribute cards are cryptic.** Label "+2" as "check bonus". Show a visible Confirm button whenever points are pending. Put a one-line explanation of a check next to the points counter.
11. **High: Room is unexplained and has three entry points.** Keep one entry. Add two sentences on what a room does, what's shared and with whom. Make the placeholder obviously fake ("e.g. ABC234") and say why Join is disabled.
12. **High: Rules starts with GM tables and maths.** Open with "How a session works in 2 minutes" for players. Move the GM card into GM mode and the formulas into an "exact maths" section. Fix the "14 adds about 2" versus "+1" contradiction.
13. **High: the quick-start guide is hidden and mismatched.** Show the role-specific half automatically after onboarding. Use names that exist on screen, since "Workshop", "GM tab" and "room button" don't.
14. **High: Build and Starting job have no descriptions.** Add one line per path on what it does and who it suits. Say whether it's permanent, and use one word for it everywhere.
15. **Medium: Combat has no obvious flow.** Add a header like "The GM sets up fights. Players see their turn here." Show a clear Start button. Explain or hide "shadows" and "Quick line".
16. **Medium: empty states are dead ends.** Gates should say "Gates are dungeons your GM opens. Nothing to do here yet." Do the same for Titles, Skills and Bag.
17. **Medium: Bestiary jargon and inconsistencies.** Explain "Extractable" and remove the "Shot · Shot" duplication. Pick tiles or feet. Fix leftovers like "AC" and "speed 20 ft".
18. **Medium: colliding terms.** Status versus Statuses, Rank versus Level, Shop versus Store, Rest versus Short rest versus Full, Hunter versus Player versus Party. Rename the clashes and add tap-for-definition on the rest.
19. **Medium: the sample character vanishes.** Don't show "Sung Jin-Woo" behind the popup, or offer "Explore a sample hunter" as an explicit choice.
20. **Medium: HP and fatigue controls are duplicated.** Keep one input per bar with a clear placeholder like "Amount". Rename fatigue "Full" to "Max out" or remove it.
21. **Medium: settings are split across Device and Data.** "Sound on" appears twice. Merge them into one Settings tab and say "this device".
22. **Medium: mobile wayfinding.** Put Rules and Room within one tap. Show the current page name when it lives under More. Keep the bar order the same in both modes, and add collapse or pagination to the long pages.
23. **Medium: Dice assumes notation knowledge.** Add a hint under the box: "2d6+3 = roll two six-sided dice, add 3". Spell out Advantage and Disadvantage, and label the floating d20 "Quick roll".
24. **Low: the "?" button and the "?" key open different things.** Make them one Help panel. Drop the footer advert for a shortcut list with two entries, and note that 1–9 can't reach ten tabs.
25. **Low: the Data page is scary for players.** Tuck export, GM key, codes and Danger zone under "Advanced". Lead with "Your character lives only in this browser. Back it up here."
