## What to build

1. **The formula field** (one component in `maker.js`, used wherever an amount is typed): ticks that build a recommended formula as text through `recommended` in core (scales with a stat, picked from the eight; aimed at Armor or at Resist; grows with a stat gap, with which two stats and a cap the field insists on; grows while channeled, linear or exponential), the text shown in a field the GM may type over; once typed over it carries an "edited" mark and a Reset that returns to the recommendation; the parser's error shows under the field in plain words and blocks Save (it refuses only what it cannot read, never a strong formula); a preview line that gives the average at ranks E, C and S against a sample target (`average` in `formula.ts` with sheets from `derive.ts`), redrawn as he types without lag (the lag test's maker case must still pass). If `recommended` cannot yet build one of those ticks, add it in core with tests in `tests/ability-kinds.test.ts`.
2. **The ability maker** (the skill maker's active part, and a monster's abilities in the enemy maker, sharing one form): the kind (strike, shot, blast, curse, support) fills everything; then each thing the GM may change, grouped and collapsed by default so the common case stays short: cost (fatigue, mana, the time it takes: quick, standard, heavy; cooldown in rounds), reach (range, the shape: touch, line with width and pierce, radius with an optional outer ring and its share), delivery (travels with a wind-up and a speed, direct so Discipline resists it, or sure; in the words a GM uses: "can be dodged", "resisted by Discipline", "cannot be avoided"), damage type (physical or magical) or heal or shield, the formula field, what it applies (status ops), and "can be channeled" (growth, the most charge). A preview that says in sentences what the ability does and how it is avoided, from core. The count of taps to make the Frost Lance skill of `ui-pass/design.md` must not rise.
3. **The status maker and the effects editor**: every op kind of `Op` in `effect-ops.ts` is offered with its fields: speed up or down by a percentage, delay, hold (stun), root, taunt, threat, shield (a formula), protect or break a channel, stagger rate, a stat up or down (which of the eight, how much), a refund (an extra-turn chance), and the damage, heal, mana and fatigue ops with their type and whether they pay each round. Each by rank (the six tiers) where the op has tiers, with a "same at every rank" tick that keeps the common case one number. `describeStatus` gives the preview. An op the editor could not edit before (W7 left some as read-only rows) is now editable; say in the notes if any op kind still is not and why.
4. **Items**: a consumable's effects through the same editor; worn gear's weight, armor, resist and a weapon's attack kind and dice, with the body's resulting speed shown for a sample hunter so "can't equip too much beefy stuff" is visible while making it.
5. **Tests** in `tests/e2e/makers.e2e.ts` (and unit tests for anything added to core), each driving the maker through the UI on a phone and reading the result from the save and from a fight: a GM ticks "scales with Power" and "aimed at Resist" and saves the recommended formula; types over it, sees the edited mark, breaks it and sees the error block Save, resets it; makes a status that slows by 30% and roots, applies it in a fight and the target's speed and movement show it; makes a blast with a ring and a 1 round wind-up, and in a fight a fast target escapes it; makes a channeled ability, and its charge grows in a fight; a shield status absorbs damage; every op kind round-trips through the maker and its parser (a loop over `Op`'s kinds, so a new kind without an editor row fails a test).
