# Notes, milestone 5: the map made right, and the extras drawn

## Differences from the brief

- Item 8, a place intent before Reveal: players see a line to the aimed tile and a dot there, not the area's outline, because the outline shows the radius, one of the numbers that stay hidden until Reveal. After Reveal (and always on the GM's phone) the outline draws.
- Item 9: players see the suggested target (a quiet line on the map, "Likely target" on the card); the threat table with its shares is on the GM's card only, behind a tap ("Threat").
- Item 10, "Staggered": the card says it from the break until the monster's pushed turn comes (`Stagger.until`), not only for a moment.
- Item 12, a player's Release travels as a `cast` of the ability the channel holds (no new room message); the GM's phone takes it as `release` only while that hunter's channel holds on its turn, so a replay or a stale tap does nothing, as for any `cast`. A player cannot pass a turn from their phone, so a player's channel grows only while the GM presses Next for them (unchanged).
- Item 12, "pips shattering": the pips burst (the channel burst of motion.ts) and go; they do not fly apart one by one.
- Item 2, "a slight lift": a target is lifted 3 px and ringed in the ability's tone; tokens it cannot hit are at 38% opacity.
