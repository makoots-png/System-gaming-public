# The lead's own pass: what the hand play found (Nov 16, at push 44)

A two-phone session played at 390 by 844 by a throwaway Playwright script: the GM copies Fester from the Workshop's kit examples, opens it in the skill maker, gives it to the player; a fight on the map with the player's hunter and two rats; the player's Strike; the rat's turn; both phones reloaded mid-fight; the fight ended and awarded with the GM's phone offline; the award arriving when it is back. Everything worked: nothing was lost, doubled or refused wrongly. What is below is look and words a player or GM meets in every fight.

| # | Where | What is seen | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | the map, both phones | A token's name is drawn under it, in the tile below. With a body on that tile the name is hidden behind that body's token (Glas over Cave Rat A: "Glas" is covered), and an aimed target's full name ("Cave Rat A") runs over its neighbour's name ("Rat B" reads "at B"). | Bodies stand next to each other in every melee; the names are then unreadable exactly when they matter. |
| L2 | the player's hotbar on their turn, 390 px | Strike, Fester, More and End turn: the fourth slot is cut at the screen's edge ("End tu", "Pass"). The bar scrolls sideways, but nothing says so, and End turn is the control used every turn. | The first screen of a player's turn hides half of the way to end it. |
| L3 | the GM's Fight tools sheet, 390 px | Width's stepper fits, Height's "+" wraps to a row of its own and reads as an unlabelled button. | A lone "+" over End encounter invites a wrong tap. |
| L4 | the GM's toast after Award XP to party while offline | "XP sent to Glas." The award is in the outbox, not sent. | The one moment the words must be true: the GM is told it went when it waits. The outbox knows (`waiting`). |
| L5 | the Encounter Complete window | "2 defeated in 0.5 rounds." and, three lines on, "Rounds fought: 0.5." | The same fact twice in one small window. Words: the wording pass may already have it. |

Not findings: the hotbar's slots, the reach's shade, the turn line, the reload (both phones came back on the fight as it stood), the offline end (one award, once).
