## The scale

- **10:** the site does not load, or every session hits it, or saves are lost for everyone.
- **8 to 9:** a common path in a real session breaks or loses a player's data or a room's messages, with no way round.
- **6 to 7:** a real session can hit it and a player sees a wrong result (a wrong number, a lost reward, a fight rule broken) or a confusing dead end; or it is rare but loses data.
- **3 to 5:** rare in a real session, or the result is wrong but easy to notice and fix by hand.
- **1 to 2:** needs a hostile or hand-edited save, a developer call, or many tabs; little effect.
- **0:** no effect a user would notice.

How the scores were set:

- Each score is what a player or GM suffers if the finding is never fixed, and how likely it is in a real session. Old saves and rooms do not need to work, so a finding that needs a save from an older build scores low.
- An architecture or state-machine finding with no symptom of its own scores 0 or 1. One that causes a scored bug gets that bug's score, and the sentence names the bug; one fix closes both.
- Most content in this app is made by the GM in the Workshop, so a finding that needs a GM-made skill or status is not rare for that reason alone. What makes it rare is a narrow combination or timing.
- "Ran" and "read" are the re-audit's: ran means a test or a browser drive showed the fault; read means it was inferred from the code.
- No finding scored 7 or more.
